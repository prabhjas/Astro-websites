In this article, we will give you all the information you need about the QIWA platform and the QIWA login. The first thing will be, to tell you that it is this new tool that was created in the kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Secondly, you will see all the steps you must take to be able to perform the QIWA login and, also, how you can relocate employees through this system.

What is Qiwa & What Role Does It Play in KSA’s Digital Transformation?

The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) built the Qiwa platform, which is an integrated digital ecosystem that brings together all services for the labor market in the Kingdom.

Qiwa is a service that brings together important services like verifying employment contracts, renewing work permits, and requesting job movement into one easy-to-use interface. It is designed for private sector companies, local and foreign workers, and business owners.

The platform’s main goals are to improve market openness, protect contractual rights, ensure legal compliance, and provide a smooth digital worker experience. It is a key part of Saudi Arabia’s digital change under Vision 2030.

If you want to create a company in the KSA or transfer staff through the QIWA platform, continue reading this guide to learn about QIWA registration, login, and employee transfer.

Note: Information in this guide is based on the official QIWA platform and Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) guidance. Always refer to the latest government announcements, as eligibility criteria and procedures may change.

What Kinds of Services Does QIWA Provide in KSA?

Qiwa brings together the whole lifecycle of your employees into a single, streamlined digital portal. It makes it easier for your business to follow the rules & its HR tasks. The tool makes it easy for employees to move between private companies and takes care of creating, updating, and digitally authenticating job contracts.

It handles important work permit services and quickly issues instant and temporary work visas to meet urgent staffing needs. Qiwa makes it easier for new or growing businesses to set up their businesses and offers ongoing help for Saudization (Nitaqat) with real-time tracking.

With live labor market tools and business compliance services, management teams can see everything at a glance. The smart e-Advisor also gives personalized advice on how to improve reviews, and thorough reports on your employees make sure that your business stays open, organized, and ready for audits.

Who Can Utilize Qiwa Services in KSA?

Qiwa is an all-in-one portal made for all the important people in Saudi Arabia’s workforce ecosystem. Full openness helps employees see verified contracts, check on the state of their work permits, and accept job moves directly through their own accounts.

Employers and business owners can use the tool to keep track of firm files, give out quick work cards, and make sure that ideal Saudization levels are always met. Qiwa makes daily tasks easier for HR professionals by automating tasks like renewing permits, keeping staff records up to date, and cutting down on administrative delays.

Foreign companies also rely on Qiwa to easily set up labor files, hire workers who follow the rules, and deal with Saudi labor laws from afar. In a modernized digital labor market, these integrated tools give all stakeholders the power to work efficiently and stay in full compliance with all regulations.

QIWA Registration Requirements in KSA

Before you can start transferring employees or using the contract tools, you need to finish setting up your account on the portal. To keep strict security and name compliance, the registration system checks your information against centralized government networks.

To make sure the setup goes smoothly, you have the following things ready:

  • A valid Absher account for online security
  • Valid ID card or Iqama number
  • Registered cell phone number that is directly linked to your Absher profile
  • Personal or business email address that works
  • Iqama expiration date in the correct wording of the Hijri calendar
  • Nafath or automated Absher SMS passcodes for two-step identity verification.

How to Register for a QIWA Account

Visiting the Official QIWA Website:

Use your browser to go to the main site at qiwa.sa for QIWA registration. Make sure you use the official government platform to keep your administrative credentials safe and to ensure that data transmission is safe.

Changing the Language (from Arabic to English)

Find the language switch in the homepage’s upper logo. If you click English, the screen will change to English, making it easier to use throughout the whole registration process.

Setting Up a New Account

On the home page, click the button that says “Register” or “QIWA sign up.” To move forward, you must choose whether you are applying as an individual worker or an approved agent of a business organization.

Enter Iqama Information

Type in your date of birth and a valid Iqama or National ID number. Make sure that the date of birth exactly matches what is recorded by the Ministry in the required Hijri format.

Verification of Mobile OTP

The platform sends a one-time identification code to the phone number you used to sign up for Absher. Quickly enter this code on the screen to prove that you are who you say you are online.

Making Login Information

When you create a QIWA account, ensure your email address and password are safe. Make sure you remember your password and choose some security questions so that you can get back into your account if you ever need to.

Setting Up the Profile

Look over the information displayed about yourself or your business, agree to the portal’s rules, and confirm. You can now use your fully created and activated Qiwa account to find official work.

How to Login to QIWA Platform

When you log in to your Qiwa account, you can use all of its digital workplace management tools. For example, you can use any device to securely view authenticated contracts, keep track of employee records, and process service transfers. Business owners and private sector workers can both get to their own dashboards through a single set of steps.

Here are some easy steps to take to log in to the platform:

  • Start up your web browser and go straight to qiwa.sa, which is the main website.
  • In the upper right part of the home page, click the “Login” button.
  • Choose the type of account you want (Qiwa Business for business owners or Qiwa Individuals for team members).
  • Enter your password and the email address or national ID/Iqama number you used to sign up. You can also choose the Unified National Single Sign-On option to use your confirmed Nafath or Absher passwords to log in.
  • Type in the one-time password (OTP) that was sent to your registered phone via SMS to finish the authentication process.
  • You can manage HR tasks right away, sign employment contracts, or look over company reports by going to your main account dashboard.

Step-by-step guide to relocating employees in KSA using QIWA

With the Qiwa employee transfer service, private companies in Saudi Arabia can easily move foreign workers from one company to another without having to do any forms by hand. Transfers are often needed to move up in a job, when a company reorganizes, or when an employee’s current contract ends.

Employees usually have 10 days to accept the offer, and their current employers have 14 days to respond. Notice periods depend on the job contract but are usually between 30 and 60 days.

To transfer personnel in Saudi Arabia using the QIWA platform, certain steps must be followed. Below we can see in detail all the steps to carry out this process:

First step: Uploading the employment contract in the QIWA KSA app

The person who is your new employer must upload an employment contract. In addition, this must be done by requesting the transfer of the services that the employee has to the QIWA service platform. However, to carry out this process, the employer must meet several requirements. The terms can be seen below:

  • According to the regulations, the employer must be able to acquire a new visa.
  • The employer demonstrated at least 80% compliance with the corresponding salary protection system. All this in the last three months.
  • The employer is ensuring full compliance with the contract registration program.
  • Similarly, the employer adheres to the maximum limit of the nationality of the QIWA KSA worker.
  • The company must adhere to the regulation of the self-assessment program in at least 80%.
  • There is a green status that serves the Nitaqar scheme for all employers.

Second step: Accept a job offer through the QIWA SA app

All applicants will be able to accept an employment contract once the employer has already launched the job portal using QIWA registration online. In the next ten days after its start, they will be able to follow the procedures to receive the work. The steps to follow to receive the job or your employment contract can be as follows:

  • All applicants must do their own QIWA.sa registration.
  • They will be able to use the QIWA login to access their accounts after successfully enrolling in one.
  • After this, they must select an individual account.
  • Go to the page and select employee transfer.
  • Press the button that says view contract.
  • You must carefully review the employment contract before being accepted into QIWA gov sa. This is because, in the eyes of the Saudi government and the ministry of human resources, it is the only one that is legally binding. Therefore, it is important that you learn about Saudi employment law and how it can help you with your employment contract.
  • You will have to accept all the conditions as they are exposed.
  • Contact the QIWA beta support service by phone or email @qiwa.sa if you have any problems. Also, you can contact us if you had any problems with your registration at qiwa.sa.
  • You can accept or reject the contract.

Standards for an organization to accept candidates

There are certain specific standards that organizations require to accept candidates. The standards are as follows:

  • Before entering Saudi Arabia with the current employer, the employee had to work for at least one year.
  • You must not have any history of unpaid violations for which you are still responsible.
  • There are no other applications open for the transfer of sponsorship.

Third step: The new employer accepts the transfer request

After all, candidates have registered and accepted the new job offer or employment contract. The new employer will receive the application and decide whether to approve or deny it. QIWA will display a status that will say pending approval by the current employer, while the employer makes its decision.

The employee transfer will be successful if the employer accepts your request within the next 14 days. However, the system may take over and automatically grant the transfer request without consent. In the same way, in both cases, the approved status will be observed in QIWA, after the QIWA app download.

If the current employer does not agree with the transfer request within the stipulated time, it is automatically rejected. In this way, the status of automatically canceled will be shown in QIWA because it is not authorized or the rejection of your request for more than 14 days.

You can automatically observe a deletion if the current employer decides to decline the transfer service request. However, the employer must present a justification for these acts.

Throughout this period, the QIWA status will be declined by the current employer. In the same way, a letter will be sent informing the employee that he will have a defense from the business owner.

Responsibilities during the employee transfer process

Each side is responsible for certain things:

Current Employer: Must quickly look over move requests, confirm contractual notice terms, and settle any outstanding end-of-service benefits.

New Employer: They need to keep their Nitaqat rating green, make sure their contract offers are legal, and pay the government transfer fees that are due.

Employee: The employee must read the new job contract carefully, digitally sign it on the Qiwa app, and give notice as planned.

Fourth step: Wait for the end of the notice period

The employee notice period in QIWA will begin as soon as your existing employer approves your transfer request. This will only happen after you have completed all the procedures mentioned above.

In this way, the existing employer will have the option of making a transfer request immediately. However, you can also postpone your dismissal until the end of the notice period. With the latter, the status that will be displayed will be pending at the end of the notification period in the QIWA sa app download.

Step Five: New Employer completes transfer

At QIWA login, the worker transfer service begins after the notice period is complete. Also, if the current employer decides to approve the transfer of the employee. The status will be changed to awaiting completion of the transfer of the new employee at Absher.

After all this, the new employer will have to pay Iqama transfer fees and make use of the Absher app. The employer can use this app to send a request to change the name of the company. You can also make the transfer of personnel in the next 24 days.

The operation will end if it does not start within 24 hours. This results in the QIWA system showing the status as expired because the new employer did not complete the application. This will be after the QIWA login.

Sixth step: Register for the new Iqama

For you to register for a new Iqama if your current employer decides to approve the transfer request, you must follow these steps:

  • Within the next 12 days, you will need to pay the cost of the transfer sponsorship to complete the Jawazat due process.
  • According to the provisions of article 40 of the Saudi labor law, the employer must cover the cost.
  • The Absher application can be used to check the progress of the new Kafeel. All this after the transfer is complete.
  • You can go to any Jawazat office to pick up the print of your new Iqama after completing the transfer.

It is important that you check your Saudi Iqama status to make sure that the whole process went smoothly. In addition, you should know about the Iqama expiry services and thus be able to avoid problems with this. Therefore, it is important that you check all of these to ensure that you do not have problems in the future.

Things You Must Know Before You Go for QIWA

There are a few important things you should remember before you start managing your labor operations or employment profile. These will help you avoid delays and other administrative problems. Because the site connects directly to national systems, you need to plan to make sure that all of your digital transactions go smoothly.

These useful tips should be kept in mind:

  • Keep your Absher information up to date. Your listed phone number and personal information must match Absher’s in order for you to get important verification codes.
  • Make sure your Iqama is still valid. You need an active, valid residency permit in order to process any official requests on the platform.
  • Carefully check employment contracts. Before digitally accepting the terms, read every sentence that talks about pay, job title, and hours.
  • Check the status of transfer requests: Keep an eye on running programs on a regular basis to make sure you can respond quickly within the required time frames.
  • Check notification deadlines: Keep an eye on approvals that are still being processed to avoid compliance flags or automatic expirations.

FAQs

What does QIWA mean in Saudi Arabia?

The Ministry of Human Resources set up Qiwa as an official digital platform to centralize labor services and make it easier to manage employment contracts across Saudi Arabia.

How do I become a member of QIWA?

Go to the official website, enter your legal Iqama information, prove who you are with an Absher SMS or OTP, and then set up your login information to finish.

How do I get into QIWA?

Click “Login” on the official website, choose the type of account you want to use, enter your passwords or unified identification information, and then verify your phone number with an OTP.

Can non-QIWA users use it?

If you have a valid Iqama, you can use Qiwa to handle your job records, contracts, and legal positions as an overseas worker, foreign investor, or owner of an international business.

How long does it take for QIWA to move an employee?

The timeline depends on contract terms, employer approvals, and notice periods.

What should I do if I can’t get into QIWA?

If you are having trouble getting in, check your internet connection, check your login details on Absher, change your password if you need to, or clear your browser’s cache.

Is Absher linked to QIWA?

Yes, Qiwa is closely linked to Absher so that users’ identities can be safely verified, residency records can be checked, and verification codes can be sent for safe account logins.
