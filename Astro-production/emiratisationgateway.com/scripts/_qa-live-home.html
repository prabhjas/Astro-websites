Emiratisation in UAE FAQ'S

What is Emiratisation?

Emiratisation is a government-driven initiative in the UAE aimed at increasing the participation of Emirati citizens in the workforce, particularly in the private sector.

Why is Emiratization important?

Emiratization is essential for promoting national talent, reducing unemployment among Emiratis, and achieving long-term economic sustainability by diversifying the labor market.

What support is available for Emirati job seekers?

Emirati job seekers can access government job portals, training programs, and career counseling services to enhance their employability and find suitable job opportunities.

What is the role of the government in Emiratization?

The government plays a central role by setting Emiratisation policies, providing financial incentives to companies, and monitoring compliance with Emiratization quotas and regulations.
