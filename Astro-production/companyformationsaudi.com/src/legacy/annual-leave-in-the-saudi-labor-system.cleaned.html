Employee Annual Leave in the Saudi Landscape

Companies and employees in Saudi Arabia need to understand and manage annual leave in accordance with the country’s labor rules and create a productive work environment. The Saudi Labor Law lays out the fundamental standards for yearly leave, making it clear who is eligible, what they are entitled to, and what rights both parties have. This article provides full guidance on these guidelines, which are necessary for a smooth procedure of managing annual leave in Saudi Arabia.

Employee Eligibility and Paid Leave Criteria in the KSA

In Saudi Arabia, the amount of leave a worker is entitled to is based on how long they have worked there. The Saudi Labor Law clarifies that workers are entitled to at least twenty-one days of paid leave each year they work. If the employee has been with the same company for five years in a row, this benefit goes up to at least thirty days. 

This tiered approach promotes loyalty and encourages people to stay with the same job for a long time. It’s crucial to remember that the law establishes a minimum, but employers can give more generous annual leave under their contract of hire. The leave is paid for in advance, which means that employees can take their time off without worrying about finances.

Various Employee Paid Leave Types in the KSA

The Saudi labor laws allow for more than just yearly paid leave. It also allows for various types of paid and unpaid time off for different life events. To have a full leave management system, you need to know these things.

Time Off for Illness:

Employees can take a maximum of 120 days off work each year for illness. There are different levels of remuneration for this:

  • Pay in full for the first thirty days.
  • For the following sixty days, you’ll get half pay.
  • Not paid for the last thirty days.
  • To get paid leave for illness, you need a doctor’s note.

Employee Time Off for Maternity and Paternity:

Women employees are entitled to ten weeks of fully paid maternal leave. When a man has a baby, he can take three days of paid paternity time off.

Employee Time Off for Hajj:

Muslim workers who have worked for the same company for two years in a row are eligible for from ten to fifteen days of paid religious vacation to go on the Hajj pilgrimage. They can only take this leave once during their time at work.

Employee Time Off for Marriage:

Employees are allowed to take five days off with pay for getting married.

Employee Paid Compassionate Leave:

They can take five days off with pay if their spouse or direct family member dies.

Leave without Pay:

The annual leave policy in Saudi Arabia lets employees take unpaid leave, but the worker and the company must agree to it.

Calculating Employee Leave and Rights in the KSA

The complete earnings of a worker include their basic earnings and any usual bonuses, which are used to figure out how much they will get paid for their yearly leave. It also ensures that an employee’s pay while they are on leave is the same as what they would make normally.

The law protects several rights of workers when it comes to their yearly leave:

Employee Right to Take Time Off:

An employee can’t give up their right to yearly leave or take funds instead of taking it while they are working. The law makes it clear that leave is not just a way to get paid more; it is also a time to rest and recharge. But if an employee’s service is ended before they have utilized all of their earned leave, they are entitled to pay for the unused leave days.

Security from Being Dismissed:

An employer cannot dismiss a worker while they are on paid leave, according to the Saudi labor laws.

Employee Postponement of Day Off:

With the employer’s written permission, a worker may postpone all or part of their yearly time off until the next year. You have to use the leave that you transferred within the next year, or you will lose it. It provides employees with some freedom while ensuring they take their earned days off.

Planning Employee Time Off:

The company can choose the dates of the time off based on the circumstances of the business, but workers must take the time off within the year it is due. The company should reconsider the worker’s request and provide them enough notice.

Most Reliable Employee Leave Management System in the KSA

It’s crucial to have a strong staff leave management system that complies with KSA Labor Law and keeps workers productive. The best systems automate the whole process, from leave request to final approval, so there is no need for manual documentation that can cause mistakes and delays. Some of the main features are tracking leave balances in real time, sending automatic reminders, and working with pay cheque and HR systems. 

A worker self-service platform makes it easy for workers to verify their annual leave and submit requests for time off. It provides employees with more control and makes things transparent. The streamlined method ensures that businesses adhere to the law while also making employees happy and keeping the workplace organized.

Paid Leave Policies: Employer and Employee Obligations in the KSA Framework

Both parties need to work collaboratively and follow the regulations for a leave management procedure to work.

Employers’ Obligations for Paid Leave:

Leave Policy and Conversations:

Employers need to have a clear and constitutional procedure for yearly leave that is readily disclosed to all employees. This policy must comply with the law and any other rules in the contract of hire.

Planning Employee Time Off:

The employer is in charge of managing leave schedules so that the business can keep running while still giving employees time off to rest.

Proper Leave Records:

Keeping proper records of every worker’s time off accrual, taken leave, and balances is crucial for complying with guidelines and being open.

Employees’ Obligations for Paid Time Off:

Notification in Advance:

Workers should ask for time off well in advance so that sufficient planning and scheduling can be done.

Paperwork:

Workers must give a proper application for certain kinds of time off, such as medical leave.

Stay Compliant:

To avoid complications, workers should understand and comply with the company’s guidelines and processes for taking time off.

FAQS

Q1. How many days of annual leave are employees entitled to in Saudi Arabia?

Employees are entitled to 21 days of paid leave annually, increasing to 30 days after five years of service.

Q2. Can employers refuse annual leave requests?

Employers can schedule leave according to business needs, but employees must be informed in advance.

Q3. Is unused annual leave carried forward in Saudi Arabia?

Yes, but only with employer approval. Otherwise, it must be compensated financially.

Q4. Can employees take annual leave in parts?

Yes, employees can split their annual leave into shorter periods with employer consent.

Q5. Are employees entitled to payment during annual leave?

Yes, employees receive full salary during annual leave, including allowances.

Q6. What happens if an employee resigns with unused annual leave?

The employer must compensate the employee for accrued but unused leave days.

Conclusion

To wrap up, understanding and adhering to the annual leave in the Saudi labor system is essential for creating a fair and effective workplace. Organizations in Saudi Arabia may ensure they comply with the regulations and create a pleasant, supportive work environment by respecting their employees’ rights, correctly calculating their leave benefits, and keeping lines of communication open.
