What is Iqama Expiry Fine in Saudi Arabia?

The Iqama is the residence permit that foreign nationals who want to live and work in Saudi Arabia must obtain. The Iqama is valid for one or five years, depending on the type of visa applied for. If the Iqama expiry fine, the resident must renew it as soon as possible, otherwise he/she will face a fine.

According to the official website of the Saudi Arabian Ministry of Interior, the fine for the expiration of the Iqama is 500 Saudi Riyals, for the first day, and 50 Saudi Riyals, for each additional day up to a maximum of 1,000 Saudi Riyals. In addition, the resident may not leave the country until he pays the fine and renews his Iqama.

The fine applies to both the employer and the employee, as both are responsible for maintaining the validity of the Iqama. Residents should regularly check the status of their Iqama expiry fine and apply for renewal at least three days before the expiration date.

  • You can renew the fine for Iqama expiry online through the Ministry of Interior’s Absher portal.
  • The resident must pay a renewal fee that varies depending on the type of visa and the category of employer. The renewal fee can range from 650 to 9,500 Saudi Riyals.

How to Check Iqama Expiry Status Online in KSA

You can know how to check Iqama expiry fine status online through the Absher portal, the Ministry of Labor (MOL) website, the Absher app or the Tawakkalna app. You will need your Iqama number, your date of birth and a verification code that will be sent to your registered phone number, to find out what is the fine for Iqama expiry.

On each of the pages there are different methods to check your Iqama expiry fine status. By following the steps set out by each of the pages or apps, you will be able to get the Iqama expiration information you need.

Using Absher Portal

To see Iqama expiry fine status on the Absher portal, you must have an Absher account, log in to the portal, go to your personal dashboard and click on “Check Iqama expiration services”. The screen will display your Iqama expiration date.

  • Visit the Absher website, select “Individuals” and log in with your username and password.
  • Once logged in, go to your personal dashboard and click on “Check Iqama expiration services” under the list of services.
  • The screen will display your Iqama expiration date. You can write it down or print it for your records.

In summary, you can check your Iqama expiration status on the Absher portal by following these simple steps. You just need to have an Absher account and your Iqama number.

How Many Days Stay After Iqama Expiry

There is no fixed number of days remaining after the expiration of Iqama. It depends on the severity of the infraction, the type of visa, the resident’s history and the discretion of the authorities. However, it is recommended to renew the Iqama as soon as possible, as a fine will be applied for each day of delay.

According to the official website of the Saudi Arabian Ministry of Interior, the fine for Iqama expiration is 500 Saudi Riyals for the first day, and 50 Saudi Riyals for each additional day up to a maximum of 1,000 Saudi Riyals. In addition, the resident will not be allowed to leave the country until he pays the fine and renews his Iqama.

The fine applies to both the employer and the employee, as both are responsible for maintaining the validity of the Iqama. Therefore, residents are advised to check the status of their Iqama regularly and apply for renewal at least three days before the expiration date.

Online Iqama renewal can be done through the Ministry of Interior’s Absher portal. The resident must pay a renewal fee that varies depending on the type of visa and the category of employer. The renewal fee can range from 650 to 9,500 Saudi Riyals.

If the resident fails to renew his Iqama within 30 days of the expiration date, authorities will deem him to have violated the residency law and subject him to additional penalties, such as deportation, entry ban, or cancellation of the visa.

In summary, the number of days remaining after the expiration of Iqama depends on several factors and can have serious consequences. Residents should renew their Iqama on time and avoid any violations.
