What is Emiratisation Target?

We explain it without the jargon. The emiratisation target is a government-backed requirement for private sector companies to hire and retain a minimum percentage of Emirati employees. It’s part of Nafis, the UAE’s national program for Emirati workforce development.

This isn’t just about hiring locals for the sake of it. The target exists to:

  • Boost Emirati participation in the private sector
  • Create sustainable career paths for local talent
  • Reduce over-reliance on expat workers
  • Strengthen economic resilience by balancing workforce diversity

Who Needs to Comply?

If you’re a private sector company with 50 or more employees, you’re in. No exemptions, no excuses.

Breaking Down the Emiratisation Target 2025

The Countdown is Real.
UAE’s government isn’t throwing this at you overnight. But the deadlines are real and non-negotiable.

Current & Upcoming Targets

  • Companies must increase Emirati hires by 2% annually for skilled roles.
  • By 2025, the goal is 10% Emiratisation in private sector firms.

Miss the targets, and you’re looking at a monthly emiratisation fine for each unfilled role not a fun expense to budget for.

Decoding the 2025 Deadline: What You Actually Need to Do

These are some things you need to do for meeting your targets. We’ve turned them into a list: 

1. The Cheat Sheet

  • Goal: 10% Emirati workforce in private-sector firms (50+ employees) by 2025.
  • Sectors in Focus: Finance, tech, healthcare, and construction.
  • Penalties: Non-compliance fines start at AED 6,000/month per unmet role.

2. The Progress Report

The UAE’s Emiratisation target 2025 is on track, but gaps remain:

  • Wins: 27,000 Emiratis joined private firms in 2023.
  • Challenges: Retention rates dip to 58% in high-turnover industries.

Use the Nafis Program platform’s subsidies to attract talent.

Why It’s Not About Avoiding Fines?

Sure, compliance is part of the story. Smart companies know there’s more to gain. Meeting those numbers opens doors to:

  • Government contracts and incentives
  • Enhanced brand reputation (local talent matters to local customers)
  • A workforce that understands UAE’s unique market culture
  • Stronger ties with regulators and business councils

It’s a chance to future-proof your talent strategy.

Emiratisation Challenges — And How to Actually Solve Them

As a leading company connecting businesses with top Emirati talent, we hear their pain points, struggles, and emiratisation challenges they’re faced with. Here, we share the solutions that we offer them too.

1. Talent Availability vs. Skills Gap

Pain Point: “We want to hire Emiratis, but they don’t apply — or they don’t have the right skills.”

Solution: Partner with programs like Nafis and local universities to tap into talent pipelines. Offer internships, training, and upskilling, so you build the talent you need.

  • Partner with UAE universities for those internships.
  • Highlight career growth paths, instead of entry-level gigs.

2. Competition for Top Emirati Talent

Pain Point: “Every company is after the same qualified candidates.”

Solution: Stand out with:

  • Competitive salaries (but don’t rely only on pay.)
  • Flexible working policies (Emiratis value work-life balance)
  • Clear career growth plans (because they’re looking for long-term roles)
  • Cultural inclusivity (where you create workplaces where Emiratis feel they belong)

3. Retention — Hiring is One Thing, Keeping Talent is Another

Pain Point: “We hired Emiratis, but turnover is high.”

Solution: Focus on:

  • Meaningful work (and avoid token hires.)
  • Mentorship programs pairing Emirati hires with senior leaders.
  • Feedback loops to understand what employees value most.
  • Recognition programs that celebrate achievements publicly.

4. Training is A Constraint Resources

Pain Point: “Training costs too much.”

Solution: Focus on:

  • Tap into government-funded upskilling programs (e.g., Emirati Human Resources Development Council).
  • Start mentorship pairings: Senior staff + Emirati hires.

Fines & Consequences

If you miss your Emiratisation target, the Ministry of Human Resources Emiratisation (MOHRE) charges a monthly fine for each unfulfilled position. This isn’t a one-time slap on the wrist; the fines stack up until you meet the quota

Don’t wait until Q4 to scramble. Build your hiring plan at the start of each year, tracking progress quarterly.

How Businesses Can Hit Emiratisation Targets Without Stress

Here’s a checklist for hitting your target without last-minute panic:

  • Understand your exact quota
    (Don’t guess, check your employee count against MOHRE guidelines.)
  • Work with an Emiratisation partner
    (Local experts who already know the talent pools and compliance rules save you time and mistakes.)
  • Get creative with recruitment
    (Attend Emirati job fairs, run referral campaigns, and build university partnerships.)
  • Build clear career paths
    Show Emiratis that joining you isn’t just a job — it’s a career.)
  • Track, report, repeat
    (Quarterly reviews keep you on track, long before the deadline hits.)

Why Emiratisation Works Best When You Strategize?

If you treat the required targets as a fine-avoidance game, you’ll always feel behind. Smart companies treat it like an investment:

  • Build relationships with local talent hubs.
  • Use Emirati hiring programs for subsidized recruitment.
  • Develop an internal Emiratisation committee to keep efforts aligned.
  • Celebrate Emirati success stories in your company, making it part of your culture.
