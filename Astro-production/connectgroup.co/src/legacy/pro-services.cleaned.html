This is why businesses stop managing PRO in the UAE themselves

The track record above only matters because of what it prevents. Here is what tends to unfold when PRO services are not in place or are handled without sufficient experience.

A visa renewal date quietly passes.

The grace period lasts 30 days and ends whether anyone is watching or not. Once it closes, the employee is legally working without valid authorisation. MOHRE flags the violation, the fine is charged per case, and a work permit freeze often follows, putting every other application your company has open on hold as well.

A document attestation is rejected.

One missing apostille or one step submitted out of sequence through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the file is sent back to the beginning. For documents issued outside the UAE, that single mistake can cost three to six weeks before the application even reaches the right desk again.

The establishment card lapses unnoticed.

The moment it expires, GDRFA stops approving new visas under your trade licence, no exceptions. Every offer letter already issued sits frozen, and there is no priority lane once this happens. You re-enter the same renewal queue as everyone else.

Your in-house PRO resigns.

Every login, every government contact, and every undocumented deadline walks out the door with them. Their replacement starts from zero, while the renewals due in that gap do not pause to let anyone catch up.

About PRO Services in the UAE

The questions every new client asks before their first brief.

What does PRO stand for and what does it cover in the UAE?

PRO stands for Public Relations Officer. In the UAE, it refers to the professional  function that manages all interactions between a company and government  authorities.  

This covers visa processing, trade licence renewals, MOHRE labour approvals,  Emirates ID, document attestation and any other official filing that requires  submission to a UAE government department.

Do I need PRO services if my company is registered in a free zone?

Yes. Free zone companies still require PRO support for employee visas, Emirates ID,  MOHRE interactions and any attestation work involving federal authorities.  

While free zones manage their own licensing, many compliance obligations still run  through the same federal systems. Connect Group handles both mainland and free  zone PRO requirements.

How is outsourcing PRO affordable than hiring an in-house PRO?

An in-house PRO in Dubai costs AED 8,000 to 12,000 per month in salary alone, plus  benefits, visa and end-of-service liability. When that person leaves, the knowledge  and the continuity go with them.  

Outsourcing to Connect Group typically costs 40 to 60% less, with no recruitment  risk, no knowledge gap and a team rather than an individual covering your account.

How long does a trade licence renewal take?

A standard DED or free zone trade licence renewal with complete documentation and no outstanding violations typically takes 3 to 7 working days once submitted.

The bigger risk is timing, not processing speed: renewal should start 30 to 45 days before expiry, since an expired licence blocks new work permits, freezes banking access and can lead to DET blacklisting if left too long. Connect Group tracks your renewal window and initiates the process well in advance so your licence never lapses.

What is document attestation and why does it take so long?

Document attestation is the process of having a document officially verified by a chain of authorities so it is recognised as legally valid in the UAE. For overseas documents this typically involves the home country’s government, the UAE embassy there and then the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

The timeline varies by country and document type, typically 2 to 6 weeks. Connect Group manages the full chain and follows up at each stage.

What is the difference between a PRO retainer and per-transaction pricing?

A monthly retainer covers all your recurring PRO tasks under one fixed monthly fee, which suits businesses with regular visa renewals, ongoing licence management and frequent government filings.

Per-transaction pricing suits businesses with occasional or one-off requirements. Connect Group advises on the right model after reviewing your annual transaction volume.
