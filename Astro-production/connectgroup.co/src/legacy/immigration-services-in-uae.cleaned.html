So what does "handling your immigration" actually mean?

Good question, because most people only find out the hard way. It is every single thing that has to happen before someone can legally work for you, starting before you even post the job, with your own company registered correctly in the immigration system. And it does not stop once they are hired. Every renewal, every transfer, every status change runs back through the same machinery, which is exactly why most businesses end up looking for UAE immigration services rather than handling it in-house.

Here is the part that catches people out: no single office owns this. Your right to sponsor someone sits with GDRFA or ICP. Whether they can legally work sits with MOHRE. Whether they are fit to be issued a visa sits with the health authority. Get one of those three wrong and the other two simply will not move, no matter how perfect everything else looks. We deal with all three so you only ever deal with us.

Your four Non-negotiables

An active establishment card, work permits issued before employment starts, accurate labour contracts and timely visa renewals are the baseline. Falling behind on any one of these puts the others at risk too.

2024: The numbers that changed

Penalties for employment violations rose from AED 50,000 to 200,000 up to AED 100,000 to 1,000,000. The window to file a labour dispute was extended to two years from the end of employment. Fictitious employment and fictitious Emiratisation now carry criminal-level fines calculated per employee, not as a single flat penalty.

What MOHRE looks for first

Three violations come up most during inspections: employing without a valid work permit, letting an establishment card lapse, and having employees on the file who do not genuinely work for the company. Under the 2024 amendment, all three now carry the highest penalty tier.

How we keep you on the right side

Every establishment card, work permit and visa under your account is tracked against its expiry and renewal window, so compliance is maintained continuously rather than discovered as a problem during an inspection.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The questions everyone asks us first

What is the difference between a work permit and a residence visa in the UAE?

A work permit comes from MoHRE and gives someone the right to work for a specific employer. A residence visa comes from GDRFA or ICP and gives them the right to live here. Most employees need both, and they run through two separate systems at the same time, not one after the other.

Does my company need an establishment card before hiring its first employee?

Yes, and there is no getting around it. Mainland companies actually need two things open before anyone can be sponsored: a GDRFA or ICP immigration card and a separate MoHRE labour file. Skip either one and a work permit or visa application simply has nowhere to go.

How many employees can a UAE company sponsor on a single licence?

There is no flat number, your visa quota depends on your office size, your business activity and your licence type. Once you hit your current limit, you can apply for an increase, usually by expanding your registered office.

Can an employee on a UAE Golden Visa still need a work permit?

Yes, and this trips a lot of people up. The Golden Visa covers residency, not the right to work. So if you hire someone who already holds one, you still have to issue them a normal MoHRE work permit before their first day. Their visa being separate from their employer does not change that.

What happens if a visa or work permit expires? Is there a grace period?

There is usually a short grace period, but treat it as a safety net, not a plan. Both employer and employee are exposed to fines once you are outside it, and renewals should start well ahead of the expiry date since processing time alone can eat into that window.

Can I process a UAE employment visa without the employee leaving the country?

Often, yes. If someone is already in the UAE on a visa that allows it, we can switch them straight to an employment visa without them flying out and back in. It depends on their current visa category and entry permit conditions, which we check before filing anything.

How long does it take to process a UAE employment visa end-to-end?

Most employment visas wrap up in 2 to 4 weeks, work permit to stamped visa, assuming the establishment card is already active and the paperwork is right the first time. When something drags on longer, it is almost always an establishment card problem or a missing document, rarely the government turnaround itself.

What are the penalties for employing someone without a valid MoHRE work permit?

Steep ones, and steeper than they used to be. Federal Decree-Law No. 9 of 2024 pushed the range from AED 50,000 to 200,000 up to AED 100,000 to 1,000,000 for employment violations like this. Fake or fictitious employment carries its own additional fines on top, charged per employee involved.

Can Connect Group manage immigration for free zone companies, not just mainland?

Yes. The establishment process looks a little different, free zone companies usually go through their own free zone’s immigration department, but medical testing and Emirates ID still run through the same federal systems either way. We handle both mainland and free zone entities

What is the difference between Connect Group's Immigration Service and PRO Services?

Immigration Services is specifically the visa and work permit side: establishment cards, permits, Emirates ID, renewals. PRO Services covers everything else you deal with the government for, trade licence renewals, document attestation, the broader admin. Most of our clients end up using both, and we deliver them as one connected service rather than two separate ones.
