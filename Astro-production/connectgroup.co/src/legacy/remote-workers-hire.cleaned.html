FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions about hiring remote workers in the UAE

Do remote workers based in the UAE need a UAE work visa?

Yes. Anyone physically working inside the UAE needs a valid work authorisation regardless of where their employer is registered. That means a MoHRE work permit and a residence visa. Connect Group provides both under its MoHRE licence as the legal employer.

Can Connect Group find the candidate as well as employ them?

Yes. Most clients come to us for both. We source and screen candidates across GCC, MENA and South Asia, present a shortlist for your approval, and then move directly into employment, visa, payroll and HR setup once you confirm your hire. You deal with one partner from the first brief to the first working day.

Is there a minimum salary for remote workers hired in the UAE?

There is no statutory minimum salary for most private sector roles under UAE Labour Law, but the salary must be sufficient to pass the WPS threshold and support a visa application.

In practice, most remote roles we place start from AED 5,000 per month for junior positions and are significantly higher for specialist and management roles. Connect Group advises on market-rate benchmarks at the brief stage.

How quickly can Connect Group source and onboard a remote worker?

For workers already in the UAE, EOR onboarding typically takes 5 to 10 working days. For overseas workers, it depends on the country: most global EOR markets complete in 7 to 14 working days. Sourcing timelines depend on the role and talent pool and are confirmed at the brief stage.

Does hiring remote workers affect Emiratisation obligations?

In most EOR structures where Connect Group is the legal employer, workers sit on our MoHRE headcount rather than yours, which can affect how your Emiratisation threshold is calculated. We advise on this specifically before any deployment, depending on your company’s current MoHRE profile.

Can an international company hire UAE-based remote workers without a UAE entity?

Yes. Connect Group’s EOR service makes this straightforward. We become the legal employer, handle MoHRE registration, visa, WPS payroll and compliance. The international company directs the day-to-day work while we carry the UAE employment liability.

Is end-of-service gratuity required for remote workers in the UAE?

Yes, for any worker employed inside the UAE under the UAE Labour Law. Connect Group calculates and tracks this from the start of the engagement and settles it correctly on exit.

How does WPS payroll work for remote workers in the UAE?

WPS is mandatory for all private sector employees inside the UAE. Salaries must go through a WPS-approved financial institution by the monthly deadline. For remote workers employed by Connect Group, we run WPS payroll and file the SIF each month. Late submission carries fines of up to AED 5,000 per employee.
