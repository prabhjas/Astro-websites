What is Global Workforce Solutions and what problem does it solve?

Global Workforce Solution lets a company legally employ, pay and manage people in countries where it has no legal entity. It brings together Employer of Record, international payroll, work authorisation, benefits and ongoing HR support across multiple markets, delivered through one partner, one contract and one point of accountability.

The problem it solves is familiar to anyone who has hired across borders. You find the right person, they live in another country, and then you discover what compliant employment there actually requires: a registered entity, a local payroll account, work permits and tax registrations. A hire that should take four weeks becomes a six-month project. Connect Group removes that entire burden. Your new employee is legally employed, fully compliant and on payroll within days, while you stay focused on the work they were hired to do.

The five things every Global Workforce
programme must get right

Global hiring fails on the same five points every time. Here is what Connect Group locks down before anyone’s first working day.

The Right Legal Structure

Before you hire in a new country, you need to know whether to use an EOR, set up a local entity or engage a contractor. Each option carries different legal and tax obligations. Getting this wrong from day one is expensive to unwind. Connect Group confirms the right structure for every role in every market before any contract is signed.

Local Payroll, Done Correctly

Paying a UAE salary to someone sitting in Germany or India does not satisfy their local payroll obligation. Each country requires payroll in local currency, income tax withheld at source and statutory contributions paid to the correct authority on time. Connect Group runs payroll under each country's own framework: WPS in the UAE, GOSI in Saudi Arabia, national schemes across Europe.

Work Authorisation, Every Market

Work authorisation differs by country, nationality, role category and employer entity status. In the GCC, visa quotas and nationalisation requirements add further layers. Connect Group manages every work permit, residency permit and employment visa across all markets it covers, with authority relationships built over nine years of regional operations and a direct filing team in-country.

Benefits and End-of-Service

Every country mandates minimum employment benefits, and the cost of missing them surfaces at the worst moment: when an employee leaves. UAE employees require end-of-service gratuity on basic salary and DHA or HAAD health insurance. Saudi Arabia requires GOSI and end-of-service. European markets require pension, statutory sick pay and paid leave entitlements that vary by jurisdiction. Connect Group tracks and administers all of it.

One HR Contact. Every Country

A team working across five countries should not experience five different levels of HR responsiveness. Connect Group assigns one named HR contact to every client engagement. That person manages all markets, all employees and all government interactions for the account, providing a single point of accountability regardless of where the work is happening or which authority needs to be dealt with.

Entity, EOR or PEO?
The 60-second answer

PEO: when you have an entity but want the compliance off your desk

You keep operational control and the employment relationship in your name. We carry payroll, statutory filings and local HR obligations alongside you, sharing the compliance load.

EOR: when you need someone hired fast, with no entity

Entity setup takes 2 to 6 months. EOR takes days. If the market is new or unproven, start here and transfer to your own entity later.

Your own entity: when you pass 40 to 50 employees in one market

At that headcount, entity costs beat EOR fees. We tell you when you reach the crossover and our company setup team handles the formation.

Contractor: only when it genuinely is not employment

Most countries apply a substance-over-form test. If the arrangement looks like employment, it gets taxed and penalised as employment. We assess every arrangement before you engage.

The four risks of getting global employment wrong

Contractor treated as employee

Back taxes, benefit liability and employment claims backdated to day one of the engagement. The exposure is retrospective, and it compounds.

Late pay, wrong deductions

AED 5,000 per employee for a late WPS run in the UAE. Compounding Saudisation errors in KSA. Dual liability across the EU.

Wrong visa, wrong category

Criminal liability in several GCC and EU jurisdictions. In the UAE, it means MoHRE fines, recruitment freezes and establishment card risk.

A hire creates a tax presence

One employee with contract authority can make your company taxable in their country. We assess PE exposure before every single hire.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about global workforce solutions, EOR, PEO and how Connect Group operates across markets.

What is the difference between an EOR and a PEO in an international context?

With EOR, Connect Group becomes the legal employer, and you need no local entity. With PEO, you keep your entity and we co-manage the employer obligations. EOR is faster to deploy. PEO gives more local control. The right choice depends on whether you already have an entity in the market.

Can a UAE company hire employees in other countries without setting up a foreign entity?

Yes. Through our Employer of Record service, Connect Group becomes the legal employer in each  market, handling contracts under local law, payroll in local currency, benefits and work  authorisation. How quickly can Connect Group onboard an employee in a new country?

UAE: within 5 working days of complete documentation. Most global markets: 3 to 7 working days. Markets requiring formal work permits take longer depending on the authority. We confirm the timeline for your specific market before engagement begins.

Does using an EOR create a Permanent Establishment risk for my company?

A correctly structured EOR does not itself create PE risk. PE arises when an employee can conclude contracts on the parent company’s behalf or a fixed place of business is habitually used. We review every engagement for PE triggers before employment begins.

How does Connect Group differ from global EOR software platforms?

Global platforms are software products that cover 150+ countries through partner networks. Connect Group is a services business with direct operations in the UAE and all GCC countries, combined sourcing and employment, and a named account manager instead of a support queue. If the UAE and GCC are your primary markets, the difference in expertise and accountability is structural, not incremental.
