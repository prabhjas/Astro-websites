Frequently Asked Questions:-

Which license category best fits my exact business activity?

We map activities against regulators, banking expectations, visa quotas, and future expansion plans before structuring.

Do you handle visas and PRO services too?

Yes. Immigration, Emirates ID, renewals, labor files, and government liaison are handled end-to-end.

Which structure should I choose: Mainland, Freezone, or Offshore?

We assess activity, banking, residency goals, taxes, and expansion plans before recommending structures.

Will I need to visit the UAE personally?

Often yes for visa processing, biometrics, and banking. We pre-arrange everything to minimize travel.

Can you assist with banking and compliance?

Absolutely. We assist with bank introductions, account preparation, regulatory filings, and can help prepare for audits separately.

What makes Connect different from agents online?

We advise strategically, price transparently, and remain accountable long after incorporation completes.
