Why HR goes wrong in the UAE?

Most UAE businesses aren’t failing at HR intentionally. They’re failing quietly — one missed deadline, one wrong calculation, one expired visa at a time

WPS field on the day it’s due, not before it

A single SIF formatting error on deadline day bounces the whole run. MOHRE fines start. Employees don’t get paid. Banks don’t answer Friday evenings.

THE PEOPLE PROBLEM

HR owned by whoever has time, not expertise

Finance processes payroll. Admin tracks visas. A manager signs contracts they haven’t properly read. Risk lives in every gap between them.

THE COMPLIANCE PROBLEM

Contracts written before the 2022 Labour Law

Federal Decree No. 33 of 2021 changed notice periods, non-competes and leave entitlements. Businesses running old contracts carry silent exposure every single day.

What HR services does Connect Group provide in UAE?

Connect Group provides end-to-end HR services across the UAE, helping businesses manage their entire employee lifecycle while remaining compliant with local regulations.

Services include:

  1. WPS payroll processing
  2. Employment contracts and documentation
  3. Recruitment and onboarding
  4. Employer of Record (EOR) services
  5. Professional Employer Organisation (PEO) services
  6. Visa and immigration support
  7. MOHRE compliance management
  8. NAFIS and Emiratisation administration
  9. HR consulting and advisory services

Support is available across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and all major UAE free zones.

Which businesses benefit most from HR services in UAE?

Any UAE business with employees. The compliance obligation is identical regardless of  size. HR outsourcing is most valuable for SMEs without a full-time HR team, international  companies entering UAE, multi-entity groups and businesses growing faster than their HR  function can keep up with.

Does Connect Group cover HR consulting in Abu Dhabi and Sharjah?

Yes. Connect Group provides HR consulting and managed HR services across all UAE, including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and all major free zones. Multi-emirate businesses work under one contract with one named contact across all locations.

What is the difference between HR outsourcing and EOR in UAE?

With HR outsourcing, you remain the legal employer, and Connect Group manages your HR operations. With Employer of Record, Connect Group becomes the legal employer. EOR suits companies entering UAE without a legal entity. HR outsourcing suits established UAE businesses that want their HR function run by specialists.

What happens if MOHRE contacts us while you are managing our HR?

Connect Group handles it. Any MOHRE inquiry, notice or inspection that relates to the HR  functions we manage is our responsibility to respond to and resolve. You forward the  communication to your named HR manager and we take it from there. This is not a  common occurrence, because proactive compliance is how we avoid it. But when it does  happen, you are not dealing with it alone.

Can you manage HR across our free zone and mainland entities together?

Yes. This is one of the most common structures we support. Free zone and mainland entities operate under different employment regulations, payroll
requirements, and compliance frameworks. Connect Group applies the appropriate processes to each entity while managing everything through a single engagement.

Clients benefit from:

  • One dedicated point of contact
  • Centralised HR management
  • Consistent reporting across all entities
  • A consolidated monthly HR overview
  • Simplified compliance administration across the business
