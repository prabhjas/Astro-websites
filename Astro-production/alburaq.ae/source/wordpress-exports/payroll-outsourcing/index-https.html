UAE Payroll Compliance Overview

Payroll operates under strict regulatory supervision in the UAE. Employers must comply with MOHRE regulations for labour contracts and wage protection. Compliance with the Central Bank guidelines for salary transfers is essential. Following the UAE Labour Law provisions for hours, leave, gratuity, and deductions are equally important.

Failure to follow these rules can result in penalties. The risk of licence suspension and restrictions on employee visas exists. The Al Buraq team has a keen knowledge of meeting compliance. Wages Protection System (WPS) Explained
Dubai & UAE

As trusted payroll outsourcing companies in Dubai, we manage both WPS and non-WPS salary processing with full compliance.

What is WPS?

The Wages Protection System is an electronic salary transfer system. The WPS was introduced by MOHRE in coordination with the UAE Central Bank. Using it helps employees receive their salaries on time and as per contract.

Who Must Comply?

  • All Mainland companies registered under MOHRE
  • Some Free Zone entities (depending on the authority)

Non-Compliance Penalties

  • Fines
  • Work permit suspension
  • Legal complications

WPS vs Non-WPS Payroll

  • WPS Payroll: Mandatory for MOHRE companies
  • Non-WPS Payroll: Applicable in certain Free Zones
