Frequently Asked Question

\n\n\n \n\n\n What is an Employer of Record and how does it help my business expand into the Middle East?

\n

\nAn Employer of Record legally hires employees on your behalf so you can expand without local setup. It helps your business expand in the Middle East as with an EOR, you don’t have to set up an entity yourself.\n

\n

\n\n\n \n\n\n How long does it take to hire and onboard employees through your EOR in Middle Eastern countries?

\n

\nIt takes only a few weeks to hire and onboard employees through your EOR in Middle Eastern countries.\n

\n

\n\n\n \n\n\n Do I need to set up a local legal entity to hire employees in the Middle East?

\n

No, you do not need to establish a local legal entity to hire employees in the Middle East when using an Employer of Record. The EOR acts as the legal employer in each country, enabling you to hire and manage employees compliantly while avoiding the time, cost, and regulatory burden of company formation.

\n

\n\n\n \n\n\n What services does your Middle East EOR solution cover?

\n

Our Middle East Employer of Record solution covers the full employee lifecycle, including compliant employment contracts, payroll processing, tax and social security management, visa and work permit support, onboarding, benefits administration, and ongoing HR and legal compliance. We ensure all employment processes align with country-specific regulations across the region.

\n

\n\n\n \n\n\n How do you ensure compliance with local labor laws across the Middle East?

\n

We ensure compliance through in-country legal experts, country-specific employment frameworks, and continuously updated compliance processes. Our team monitors labor law changes, payroll regulations, tax obligations, and immigration requirements in each Middle Eastern country to keep your business fully compliant at all times.

\n

\n\n\n \n\n\n Which Middle Eastern countries do you support with Employer of Record services?

\n

We provide Employer of Record services across both GCC and non-GCC Middle Eastern countries, including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, and others. Our regional coverage allows you to hire employees in multiple countries using one trusted EOR partner.
