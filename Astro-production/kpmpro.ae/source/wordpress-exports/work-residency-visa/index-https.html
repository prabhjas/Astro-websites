Due to its comparatively easy visa requirements in comparison to neighboring Middle Eastern nations, the United Arab Emirates, is a top location for expats. Because of this, the nation is a fantastic option for businesses that are growing worldwide. However, just like in any other nation, particular guidelines must be met while applying for work permits, residence visas, and immigration.

KPM PRO will help you navigate through the process of providing workers with the necessary work permits and residency visas to start working in the UAE.

Required Document's for Work & Residency Visa

To process the residency visa, any businesses that intend to hire employees must possess the following:

  • Legitimate business license.
  • Card for immigration and establishment.
  • Original emirates ID of sponsor
  • E-Signature Card.
  • Online Account for Immigration (not mandatory).

On the other hand, if you are an employee, all you need to do is obtain the necessary documents include:

  • Copy of the valid passport.
  • Passport pictures.
  • Emirates ID card.
  • Visa Page.
  • Visa Cancellation
  • MOFA Attested degree
  • Pakistani ID for Pakistan’s Nationalist

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the process for obtaining a UAE work and residency visa for a new employee?

The process for getting a work and residency visa for a new employee in the UAE involves the employer applying for the work permit, entry permit issuance, medical testing, Emirates ID registration, and visa stamping on the employee’s passport.

What are the medical fitness test requirements for UAE residency?

For UAE residency, the applicant needs to pass a medical test screening for infectious diseases such as tuberculosis and HIV.

What documents does an employer need to sponsor an employee's UAE residency visa?

Employee’s passport, photographs, job offer or contract, company trade license, and immigration establishment card are the documents that an employer needs for sponsoring their employee’s residency visa.

How many employees can a Dubai mainland company sponsor on work visas?

Office size, business activity, and approvals from relevant authorities determine the quota for the number of employees a Dubai mainland company can sponsor.

What are the salary requirements for sponsoring a UAE work visa?

There is no strict minimum salary for a work visa but it must accurately reflect the job role and UAE labour regulations.

What is the difference between an employment visa and a residency visa?

An employment visa allows entry for work purposes whereas a residency visa grants legal stay and is stamped after completing formalities.

Can I sponsor a work visa for someone already inside the UAE on a different visa?

You can sponsor a work visa for someone who’s already inside the UAE on a different visa.
