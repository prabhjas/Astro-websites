    FAQ’s

    FREQUENLY QUESTION

    What is a multi-entry visa?

    During the legitimacy time frame, a multi-entry visa permits you to enter a country on various occasions. Not at all like a single-entry visa which allows just a single passage during the legitimacy time frame.

    How much does a multi entry Dubai visa cost?

    The expense of a multi-entry Dubai visa relies upon the validity. The short-term multi-entry visa is for 30 days. It is around AED 690. Then again, the long-term multi-entry visa is substantial for 90 days. It is around AED 1740.

    What is the visa issued for multiple entries?

    A visa issued for multiple entries is the same as a multi-entry visa. Within the validity period, it allows you to enter the issuing country multiple times. Unlike a single-entry visa which only grants entry once.

    What is a multiple re-entry visa?

    A multiple-entry visa is another term for a multi-entry visa. This visa allows you to exit and re-enter the issuing country multiple times. You can enter during the visa’s validity period. However, keep in mind you can enter as long as you meet any specific conditions. This includes not exceeding a certain total duration of stay.

    How can I get a multiple entry visa for the UAE?

    For the UAE, there isn’t an available multi-entry visa. However, several visa options allow for multiple entries depending on your situation. The first one is a tourist visa which offers 5-years duration. The second is an Investor visa which offers a 5-year duration 2 years and 10 years and comes with multiple entry privileges.
