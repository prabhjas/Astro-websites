    FAQ’s

    FREQUENLY QUESTION

    What are freezone companies in the UAE?

    In the UAE, free zone companies are businesses set up in designated areas. They offer tax benefits, streamlined business processes, and full foreign ownership.

    How much does a free zone company cost in UAE?

    The cost of a free zone company in the UAE can vary. However, it depends on the:

    • Specific free zone
    • license type, and
    • Number of visas needed. Generally, it starts at around AED 10,000 which is USD 2,720 annually.
    What is the difference between LLC and Freezone in UAE?

    LLCs in the UAE mainland allow majority foreign ownership with a local partner. On the other hand, free zones offer 100% foreign ownership but limit operation to the free zone area.

    How to open a Dubai Free Zone company?

    To open a Dubai Free Zone company, follow the steps below:

    • Choose your business activity
    • Select the free zone
    • Apply for a license through the chosen free zone authority.
    Can Freezone company buy property in Dubai?

    Only Freezone companies registered in Dubai can buy property within designated areas of Dubai. Those from other Emirates can’t buy. However, there are some exceptions, such as RAKICC companies with a recent MOU.
