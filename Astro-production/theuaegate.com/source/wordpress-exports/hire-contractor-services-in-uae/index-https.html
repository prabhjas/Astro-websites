    Employees are paid at least once a month through the WPS. It is regulated by MOHRE. On the other hand, independent contractors are paid once they have completed their task.

    What is the contractor tax in Dubai?

    Individuals are not required to pay tax on their earnings. On the other hand, a corporate tax law was introduced in 2023. According to this, contractors must pay 9% tax on any income over 375,000 AED.
