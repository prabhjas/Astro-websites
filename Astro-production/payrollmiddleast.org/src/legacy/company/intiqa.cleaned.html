Leading Payroll Service Providers

At Intiqa we offer our clients in payroll outsourcing services, which can be complemented with the hiring of personnel. Since 2011, our company has offered complete services of the best quality in the region through best practices. In this sense, we have a combination of experience in the business area locally and globally.

Our organization assists you in payroll processing under the labor and tax regulations and laws of the country. We are leaders in the nation as we provide our payroll services to some of the best companies in the world. Our clients include IBM, General Electric, Nokia, Shell, and many others.

We assure our clients of the implementation of payroll under national standards and in compliance with laws. We offer a transparent service, with which we generate detailed reports and receipts authorized by the tax and social security departments. Our payroll storage and processing technology is intiPAY, which is cloud-based for added security.

Thanks to this technology, both the employer and employees have access to the payroll with a simple interface. They will be able to access tax summaries, individual payroll, social security, and other relevant reports. This way, both payroll processing and reports are accurate and generated in a short time.

Our work with you will cover every aspect of payroll, from salary calculation to delivering payroll to your employees. We guarantee accuracy in payroll and compliance with all legal requirements in the country.
