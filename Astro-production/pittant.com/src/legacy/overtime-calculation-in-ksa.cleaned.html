What Is Considered Overtime Under Saudi Labor Law

Overtime is any work performed beyond 8 hours per day or 48 hours per week as per the Saudi law. The overtime is calculated differently in Ramadan. Overtime applies to work exceeding 6 hours per day for Muslim employees during the Holy month. Overtime must be paid at 150 percent of the worker’s basic hourly wage. Employers can’t require overtime without the worker’s consent. The exception is in limited cases.

Definition of overtime in KSA:

Saudi Labor Law defines:

Overtime as any work performed beyond the maximum daily or weekly working hours. Overtime pay becomes a legal obligation when an employee crosses these limits. It does not serve as a benefit.

Daily vs weekly overtime thresholds:

An employee is eligible for overtime pay when they work more than 8 hours in a day or 48 hours in a week. Crossing the weekly limit still triggers overtime, even if daily hours seem normal.

Difference between regular hours & overtime hours:

In Saudi Arabia, the regular hours fall within legal limits. Overtime hours start immediately after those limits end. It may also require higher compensation.

Role of employment contracts in overtime eligibility:

Employment contracts may define schedules. However, they cannot override the Saudi Labor Law. Overtime applies regardless of contract wording if actual work exceeds legal limits.

Standard Working Hours Regulations in Saudi Arabia:

Standard working hours in KSA are limited to eight hours per day or forty-eight hours per week. The Holy month of Ramadan follows different schedules. The working hours for Muslim employees are reduced to six hours per day or thirty-six hours per week in the sacred month. However, employers must provide rest breaks. They are obligated to give at least one full weekly day off. Any work beyond these limits is considered overtime.

Normal working hours:

Saudi law sets working hours at 8 hours per day or 48 hours per week. Employers must structure schedules within these limits.

Weekly rest day rules:

Every employee must receive at least one paid rest day per week. It is usually Friday when Saudi Arabia follows a day off, unless the job requires otherwise.

Breaks, prayer times, and exclusions:

Meal breaks and prayer times do not count as working hours if employees are free from work duties.

Maximum daily working hours:

Labor law restricts excessive daily hours, even with overtime. Continuous overwork can trigger violations during inspections.

Important note:

Enforcement and payroll audits are becoming more frequent in 2026. This makes accurate tracking essential.

Working Hours During Ramadan:

The working hours in Ramadan are changed that that of regular days and months. The Saudi labor law offer muslim employees to work less than they usually do on regular days.

Reduced working hours for Muslim employees:

During Ramadan, Muslim employees work a maximum of six hours per day or thirty-six hours per week. The leverage is often provided to muslim employees so that they can pray and make the best during their fast.

Ramadan schedule for non-Muslim employees:

The timings remain 8 hours per day for non-Muslim workers during Ramadan. Non-Muslim employees usually follow regular schedules unless the employer applies a unified Ramadan policy.

When overtime applies during Ramadan:

Any hour worked beyond the reduced Ramadan limits by employees counts as overtime. If this happens, it must be paid at overtime rates.

Common employer mistakes during Ramadan payroll:

Employers often make a common mistake during Ramadan. They forget to adjust overtime thresholds during Ramadan. This may lead to underpayment and disputes.

Overtime Calculation Formula in Saudi Arabia: Steps Involved

Saudi law applies a 150 percent overtime rate. Let’s understand how it works.

How to Calculate the Hourly Wage

Monthly Salary ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = Hourly Wage

This formula standardizes wages across payroll systems.

How to Calculate the Overtime Rate

Hourly Wage × 1.5 = Overtime Rate

This reflects the legally required overtime premium.

Final Overtime Pay Formula

Overtime Rate × Overtime Hours = Total Overtime Pay

This method aligns directly with Saudi Labor Law requirements.

Practical Overtime Calculation Examples

Example: Weekday overtime calculation

Monthly salary: Riyal 6,000
Hourly wage: 6,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = Riyal 25
Overtime rate: 25 × 1.5 = Riyal 37.5
Overtime hours: 8
Overtime pay: 8 × 37.5 = Riyal 300

Example: Weekend overtime calculation

Employee works 6 hours on a rest day
Hourly wage: Riyal 25
Overtime rate: Riyal 37.5
Overtime pay: 6 × 37.5 = Riyal 225 + daily wage

Example: Public holiday overtime calculation

Employee works 8 hours on a public holiday
Receives full daily wage + 8 hours at 150 percent
Total compensation equals nearly double pay

Example: Over time, during Ramadan

Ramadan daily limit: 6 hours
Employee works 8 hours
Extra hours: 2
Overtime rate applies to both hours
Overtime pay: 2 × hourly wage × 1.5

When Is Overtime Pay Mandatory?

Overtime has become mandatory to pay when your workers work more than the regular daily and weekly working hours. They are also eligible to ask for overtime when they work on weekends, on holidays, and on night shifts. As an employer, it’s your responsibility to make payments on time so that you can avoid any future disputes.

Exceeding daily working hours:

Any work beyond 8 hours requires overtime pay.

Exceeding weekly working hours:

Crossing 48 hours per week triggers overtime even if daily limits appear normal.

Working on weekends:

Work on rest days must include overtime compensation.

Working on official public holidays:

Holiday work always qualifies for additional pay.

Night work (10 PM – 6 AM):

Night work often involves overtime unless part of an approved shift system.

Article 107 clearly states that employers must pay overtime in these cases.

Overtime Pay for Weekends & Public Holidays:

Overtime pay calculation is different when your workers work on their weekends and public holidays. Similarly, the Saudi labour law mentions clearly the difference between the rest day pay and overtime pay. For instance, if a worker is supposed to do work on a holiday, they’ll get a chance to earn double compensation.

How weekend overtime is calculated:

Employees receive their normal daily wage plus overtime for each hour worked.

How public holiday overtime is calculated:

Holiday work includes regular pay plus 150 percent overtime for hours worked.

Difference between rest day pay and overtime pay:

Rest day pay covers the day. Overtime pay covers the hours.

Why holiday work often equals double compensation:

Combining daily wage and overtime rate results in nearly double earnings.

Who Is Exempt from Overtime Pay in Saudi Arabia:

In Saudi Arabia, many high-level executives are exempted from the list. Managers and supervisors do not fall in the category of overtime pay. Sales and field workers also do not qualify for overtime. Employers are not required to provide overtime pay to flexible and special contract employees. This category is also exempt from the list.

Managers and supervisors:

Employees with real authority over hiring and discipline may be exempt.

Sales and field employees:

Roles performed outside offices often follow special rules.

Flexible or special contract employees:

Eligibility depends on contract structure and actual job duties.

Importance of role definition:

Poorly defined roles increase legal risk and misclassification penalties.

FAQs

What is the overtime rate in KSA?

Saudi Law sets overtime pay at 150 percent of the worker’s regular hourly wage.

How is the hourly wage calculated for overtime?

Divide the monthly salary by thirty days. Then divide by eight working hours.

Is overtime essential during Ramadan?

Yes. Any work beyond reduced Ramadan hours qualifies as overtime.

Are weekends and public holidays considered overtime?

Absolutely!

Work on rest days or holidays requires overtime compensation. It is an additional pay than the regular one.

How is the basic salary used to calculate the value of an employee’s annual leave in Saudi Arabia?

To determine annual leave pay, the basic salary is divided by thirty days to find the daily rate.

Can annual leave be split into parts? What are the conditions for this in Saudi Arabia?

Yes!

Saudi employees can split leave if approved by the employer. This workflow ensures continuity and mutual agreement.

What happens to unused annual leave when an employee’s service ends in Saudi Arabia?

At termination, unused leave is converted into cash compensation based on the employee’s basic salary.

Are there regulations on when the employer must approve or schedule annual leave for an employee in Saudi Arabia?

Saudi employers must approve leave, considering business needs. However, workers should request it in advance for approval.

Does Saudi labour law allow cash-out of annual leave while the employee is still in service?

Employees can cash out unused leave. Given that, it’s agreed upon with the employer and documented properly.

How many leave days is an employee entitled to after more than five years of continuous service with the same Saudi employer?

After five years of continuous work, your workers are eligible for thirty days of annual leave.

What types of leave other than annual leave might affect or reduce the accrual of annual leave in Saudi Arabia?

Various leave types can directly affect the annual leaves, including:

  • Sick leave

  • Long-term absence.
