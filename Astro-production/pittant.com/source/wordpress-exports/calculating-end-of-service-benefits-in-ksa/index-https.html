FAQs

Who is eligible to receive end of service gratuity in Saudi Arabia?

Most full time employees under the Saudi Labor Law are eligible once they complete the required period of service. Eligibility depends on contract type, duration and reason for leaving.

How is end of service gratuity calculated according to Saudi labor law?

It is calculated based on an employee’s final basic salary and total years of service. The law outlines specific formulas for the first five years and the years that follow.

What role does basic salary play in calculating gratuity in KSA?

The basic salary is the foundation of the calculation. Allowances and variable benefits are not included, so accurate payroll structure is essential.

How does resignation affect end of service benefits in Saudi Arabia?

If an employee resigns, their gratuity may be reduced depending on how long they worked. Full benefits usually apply after completing a longer service period.

What happens to gratuity if an employee is terminated?

If the termination follows Saudi labor regulations, the employee typically receives full gratuity. However, misconduct related termination can impact eligibility.

How can HR departments simplify end of service gratuity calculations in Saudi Arabia?

They can use automated HR or payroll tools that apply the official formulas correctly, reduce manual errors and maintain updated records for every employee.
