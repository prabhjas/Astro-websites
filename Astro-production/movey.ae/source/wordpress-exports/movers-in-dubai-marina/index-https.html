Obtaining Quotes and Estimates:

When you are choosing the movers in the Dubai Marina, the process of requesting quotes and estimates is vital.

  • List Your Inventory: Make sure all the items you are moving are on your list. Hence, the movers do a more specific job and thus issue a more accurate estimate.
  • Provide Details: Provide details concerning the relocation such as, the date and favorite location, in order to get correct quotations.
  • Ask for In-Home Estimates: At any given moment, please ask for a home estimate if you can.
  • Understand the Pricing Structure: Identify whether the quotation is firm or non-binding. Also, indicate what operational factors may cause any price variation.
  • Inquire About Additional Services: Meanwhile, inquire the additional charges for services such as packing, storage and special handling.
  • Review the Estimate Details: Go ahead and check the estimate, after you have acquired it. Look in the document again to see that all services and fees are there.

Confirming Services and Terms:

  • Review the Contract: Take a good and close look at the contract that the movers gave you.
  • Clarify Services: Ensure that you get all the services you need in the contract like packing, loading, unloading, and unpacking.
  • Understand the Pricing Model: The billing system should be clear to you. Whether the rate is flat or hourly, and other charges. For example, the delay or extra service.
  • Check for Additional Fees: Along the usual fees, take into account any possible additional charges that may apply. For example, for high weight lifting, tight spots, last minute changes, etc.
  • Inquire About Insurance: Know the kind of insurance that protect your belongings while you move.
  • Discuss Scheduling: Note the date and time when you are moving, and how long it will probably be. Also, the time it will take.
  • Ask About Cancellation Policy: Get to know the policy of cancelling or rescheduling the move and the date in case of late cancelation.

  • Finalize the Agreement: Bearing in mind that all services and terms are clear, it is time to finish the contract. Sign the contract when you are sure you understand all its conditions and that they are ok for you.
