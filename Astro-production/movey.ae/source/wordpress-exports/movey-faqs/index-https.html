General
Can I schedule my move in advance?

Absolutely! We encourage our customers to plan ahead. Once you confirm your move with us, we will work with you to set a convenient date and time for your relocation.

Are packing materials provided, or can I get them in advance?

We offer packing materials and boxes as part of our comprehensive moving service. If you prefer to pack some personal items yourself, we can happily deliver the materials to you in advance.

Does Movey provide packing services?

Yes, indeed! Our skilled team of movers can expertly pack your belongings, ensuring they are protected and ready for the journey.

Do you provide transportation for specialty items like pianos or artwork?

Yes, we specialize in moving delicate and valuable items, including pianos, artwork, and antiques. Our team is equipped to handle such items with utmost care.

What areas does Movey cover for relocations?

Movey operates in Dubai and serves various areas within the city and its surroundings.

Are your moving services available for both residential and commercial relocations?

Absolutely! Movey caters to both residential and commercial moves, ensuring a seamless and efficient experience for all our customers.

Can I make changes to my moving date after booking?

We understand that plans can change. Please inform us at the earliest, and we will do our best to accommodate any necessary changes to your moving date.

How do you ensure the safety of my belongings during the move?

At Movey, the safety of your belongings is our priority. We use covered trucks, providing protection from head damage, and our movers handle items with care and precision.

What is the best time to schedule a move with Movey?

We prioritize flexibility and strive to find the most convenient time for your move. Our services are available on both weekdays and weekends, allowing us to accommodate your schedule seamlessly.

How do I book Movey’s services?

Booking with Movey is easy! You can contact us through our website, email, or phone, and our friendly team will guide you through the process and answer any questions you may have.

Are your movers licensed and insured?

Yes, all our movers are licensed professionals, and we are fully insured to provide you with peace of mind during your move.

What sets Movey apart from other moving companies in Dubai?

At Movey, our dedication to customer satisfaction, reliable services, and attention to detail make us stand out as the preferred choice for moving in Dubai.
