Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with your interior design services?

Getting started is easy! Simply reach out to us through our website or contact us directly to schedule an initial consultation. During this consultation, we’ll discuss your project goals, budget, and timeline to create a customized design plan that aligns with your vision.

What is the typical timeline for an interior design project?

The timeline for each project varies depending on its scope and complexity. We’ll provide you with a detailed project timeline during the initial consultation, outlining key milestones and anticipated completion dates to ensure transparency and clarity throughout the process.

How much does interior design services cost?

The cost of our interior design services varies depending on the scope of the project, size of the space, and specific requirements. We offer flexible pricing options to accommodate a range of budgets and will provide you with a detailed proposal outlining the costs involved before any work begins.

Do you work with existing furniture and decor, or do I need to purchase new items?

We understand the importance of incorporating your existing furniture and decor into the design whenever possible. Our team will work closely with you to assess your current belongings and determine how they can be integrated into the new design. If additional items are needed, we can provide recommendations and assist with sourcing them.
