01State & Federal Registration Setup

From EIN setup to state unemployment insurance and withholding accounts, your business remains fully compliant before the first payroll run. This eliminates setup errors that trigger audits.

02 Gross-to-Net Salary Calculation

We calculate every paycheck with accuracy, from federal to local requirements.

Federal income tax, FICA (Social Security & Medicare), SALT deductions, and strict adherence to FLSA overtime rules are included. Every employee is paid accurately and lawfully.

03 Automated Tax Withholding & Remittance

The payroll tax management process makes your every tax obligation correct and timely.

We automate deposits by using advanced payroll tax management software. File Forms 940 and 941, and manage FUTA/SUTA obligations. Your business stays compliant and audit-ready.

04 US Benefits & Deductions Administration

Complex employee deductions are our duty to handle without disrupting payroll accuracy.

From 401(k) contributions and ACA-compliant health insurance premiums to HSA/FSA accounts and wage garnishments, we look after each deduction. It’s either pre-tax or post-tax.

05 Year-End Reporting & Issuance

Closing your payroll year without stress or delays becomes possible!

Our system lawfully generates and distributes W-2s and 1099-NECs. Throughout, we maintain compliance with IRS deadlines, eliminating year-end bottlenecks.

Frequently asked questions

Here’s what our clients usually ask us

How do you handle multi-state payroll tax management?

Our system calculates taxes based on where employees live and work. This includes:

  • State income tax
  • Local city taxes, and
  • State unemployment insurance (SUTA).

We monitor every change on a regular basis. Compliance is maintained within all states. The risk of filing errors or underpayments has been reduced.

Can your online payroll management system process W-2s and 1099s?

Kinza HR’s online payroll management system supports employees and independent contractors. We manage different pay structures, taxes, and reporting requirements. Correct processing yearly and the generation of W-2 and 1099-NEC forms is guaranteed.

Does your HR payroll management system assist with FLSA compliance?

Our HR payroll management system tracks hours worked and applies the correct pay rates. We calculate time-and-a-half overtime automatically as required by the FLSA. It also maintains detailed records. So that you stay prepared for Department of Labor audits or disputes.

Will you file our quarterly Form 941 and annual taxes?

We handle full-service tax filing. This includes quarterly Form 941, annual filings, and all required federal, state, and local submissions. Our payroll tax management process provides timely deposits. Always deliver accurate filings and complete compliance with IRS regulations.

Why should we outsource to a US payroll management company?

The US payroll scene is highly inconsistent, with tax obligations constantly changing. Team up with a specialized payroll management company to reduce the risk of IRS penalties, compliance errors, and employee disputes. Kinza HR never disappoints in fulfilling its obligations. Your internal teams can utilize their time best to focus on your growth.
