Frequently asked questions

Here’s what UAE employers usually ask us

What are outplacement services in the UAE?

Outplacement services in the UAE help employees move smoothly from one role to the next after leaving a company. These services are usually arranged by employers and can include career coaching, CV improvement, LinkedIn profile support, interview guidance, and practical advice on finding opportunities within the UAE job market, especially during visa-related transitions.

How much do outplacement services cost in the UAE?

The cost of outplacement services in the UAE can vary based on the level of support required, the number of employees involved, and whether the program is designed for junior staff or senior leadership. Some companies choose flexible per-person packages, while others prefer long-term transition support programs tailored to their organization.

Are outplacement services offered online or face-to-face?

Most outplacement services in the UAE are available both online and in person. Employees can attend virtual coaching sessions, access digital career resources, and receive remote support from anywhere. In-person meetings and workshops can also be arranged for organizations that prefer a more hands-on approach.

How long does outplacement support usually continue?

The duration of outplacement support depends on the employee’s role, career goals, and current market conditions. In the UAE, many programs run for around three to six months, although some organizations may extend support for senior professionals or specialized roles.

Do outplacement services help with finding jobs in the UAE?

Yes, many outplacement programs are designed specifically to help professionals understand the UAE hiring market. This can include guidance on recruiter expectations, networking strategies, interview preparation, and improving personal branding to increase job search success across the region.

Can outplacement support help during UAE visa transitions?

Yes. Since employment and residency visas in the UAE are linked to employment status, outplacement support often includes guidance during the transition period. This helps employees manage their job search more confidently while navigating timelines related to visa changes or sponsorship transfers.
