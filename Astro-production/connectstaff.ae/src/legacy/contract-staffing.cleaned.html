Contract Staffing Agency in Dubai, UAE

Contract staffing in Dubai allows companies to hire workers under a particular agreement. This means that the workers will work for a certain company until the contract is fulfilled. In this agreement, the company will pay a particular salary to the worker until the project is completed. Our staffing services in Dubai can help you find professionals that can be ideal for your long term or short term employment contracts.

    FAQ

    How does contract staffing work in the UAE?

    The contract staffing in the UAE progresses as hiring of the new candidates by the company for the specific period of time and for a particular purpose. 

    Are there any legal requirements for contract staffing in the UAE?

    Yes, there are legal needs that were imposed by the UAE government under Labour law. The key elements would be a written contract in Arabic language with English translation, valid work permit and residency visa and giving wages to the employees under Wage Protection System ( WPS). Other than this, according to a new revision to the labor law, the contract staff should be hired for a fixed-term, not exceeding three years. However, the duration can be shortened.

    What rights do contract workers have in the UAE?

    The contract employees have similar rights as any other worker of the company as imposed by UAE Labour Law. The rights include the formal written contract between employee and employer, paid leaves entitlement, justified wages, end-of-contract gratuity right, termination right, safe and sound environment etc.

    Can contract workers in the UAE be hired permanently?

    The contract workers in the UAE can be transformed into a permanent employee if they have completed their fixed term contract. This transition depends on the employer satisfaction with their work
