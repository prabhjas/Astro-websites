FAQ’s

For Employers

What types of positions can you help fill?

Across various industries, we specialize in filling several positions including administrative, IT, healthcare, finance, engineering, manufacturing, and more.

The Connect Staff provides talent for entry-level roles to executive positions.

How do you examine candidates?

The Connect Staff follow a step-by-step process to examine candidates: The steps are:

  • Thorough review of resumes
  • Skills assessments
  • Background checks
  • Interviews

Our team ensures that candidates meet the qualifications and experience required for the roles we fill.

How long does it take to fill a position?

It depends on the job requirements and market conditions to fill a position. We often provide candidates quickly for common positions. But, specialized or senior roles may take longer. Our team works closely with clients to meet deadlines.

What are the costs associated with using your services?

The service fees vary from case to case. It depends on the service type and the position level. Connect Staff offers competitive pricing, keeping in mind the client’s budget. We provide a detailed cost breakdown before starting the recruitment process.

What if a candidate doesn’t work out?

Don’t worry about it! By hiring our service, you will get guaranteed satisfaction. You can get a replacement if a candidate doesn’t meet your expectations.

Can I hire a temporary worker permanently?

Absolutely! In the past, our several clients have transitioned temporary workers to permanent roles. Connect Staff is offering temp-to-perm arrangements. This allows you to assess a candidate’s fit before making a hiring decision. Note that, you may incur a conversion fee. The fee is already outlined in your service agreement.

Do you provide payroll and benefits administration for temporary employees?

For temporary and contract placements, we handle:

  • Payroll
  • Taxes, and
  • Benefits Administration

It includes weekly or bi-weekly paychecks. Also, it complies with tax regulations while offering benefits as applicable.

What industries do you specialize in?

The Connect Staff serves a wide range of industries. This includes but is not limited to:

  • E-Commerce Industry
  • Hospitality Industry
  • Real Estate & Construction
  • Energy & Chemicals
  • HR and Business Support
  • Retail & Trading Industry
  • Financial & Banking
  • Information Technology
  • Shipping Industry
  • FMCG Industry
  • Manufacturing Industry
  • Supply Chain and Logistics
  • Healthcare Industry
  • Oil and Gas

We have recruiters who have industry-specific expertise to understand your unique needs.

How do you ensure compliance with labor laws?

Connect Staff is committed to following all labor laws. Our team stays updated with the latest regulations. We ensure that all practices adhere to legal requirements.

For Jobseekers

How do I apply for a job through your agency?

To apply for a job through our agency website, follow the process:

  • Browse our job listings on our website
  • Submit your application for the positions that match your skills and interests.
  • Upload your resume

After submitting the details, our team will match you with suitable opportunities.

What types of jobs do you offer?

Across various industries, we offer a range of job opportunities. This entails full-time, part-time, temporary, and contract positions. Our listings include roles in sectors:

  • E-Commerce Industry
  • Hospitality Industry
  • Real Estate & Construction
  • Energy & Chemicals
  • HR and Business Support
  • Retail & Trading Industry
  • Financial & Banking
  • Information Technology
  • Shipping Industry
  • FMCG Industry
  • Manufacturing Industry
  • Supply Chain and Logistics
  • Healthcare Industry
  • Oil and Gas
Do I need to pay any fees to apply or use your services?

No, you don’t have to pay. Our services are free for job seekers. Connect Staff compensated by employers who hire our candidates.

How long does the screening process take?

The screening process can vary from case to case. It depends on the position and employer.

Upon submitting your application, our team will review it.

If you are shortlisted, we will contact you for further steps. The additional steps include interviews or assessments.

Can I apply for multiple positions?

No, you cannot apply for multiple positions at the same time. The Connect Staff doesn’t encourage such practice to apply for several positions at once.

What should I expect during the interview process?

Our interview process involves an initial screening. It can be done by our recruitment team. The process is further followed by interviews with potential employers. The interview process may include:

  • In-person
  • Virtual, or
  • Phone interviews.

It could involve skill assessments. This depends on the job requirements.

How do you protect my personal information?

We take care of everything, especially when it comes to confidentiality.

The Connect Staff prioritizes the security of your personal information.

Our team follows strict data protection policies. They only share your information with employers with your consent.

Can I update my resume or contact information?

If you want to update your resume and miss some important details. There is an option to re-submit your resume.

Do it anytime by logging into your account on our website. We advise you to keep your profile current.

It helps us match you with the best opportunities.
