    FAQ

    What roles can event staffing agencies provide for events in the UAE?

    The event staffing agency works as an event management specialist that offers multiple services like Staff Recruitment, Staff Training, Staff Scheduling and Deployment, On-Site Management and Staff Evaluation. It provides staff various roles like event host, brand ambassador, ushers, greeters etc.

    Are there specific requirements for event staff in the UAE?

    Although, there are no particular requirements for the event staff in the UAE. but few key factor sthat should be taken into consideration would work permit visa that foreign employee must have. other than this, for particular location or nature of the event would require permission. some workers needs extra certifications based on their specific tasks. A written contract in Arabic, defining employment term is also suggested.

    How far in advance should event organizers book staffing services in the UAE?

    Depending on the scale and complexity of the event, the best time to schedule event staffing in the UAE is usually two to four months in advance. If the event is bigger or more complex, reservations should be made even six months in advance.

    Can event staffing services in the UAE provide staff for multiple-day events?

    Definitely yes! If you need extra helping hand or complete support to manage an event, the professional team can assist for as many days as you want.

    What factors should event organizers consider when choosing an event staffing agency in the UAE?

     The factors that every event organizer should consider while choosing an event staffing agency can be:

    • check if their expertise matches with your event’s requirement s
    • inquire if their staff or members are qualified and proficient enough for their roles.
    • make sure the agency is trust-worthy enough to send the demanded number of staff. that too, on time. 
    • Get a clear understanding of the agency’s pricing structure. 
    • Get the information and reviews about the past events managed by this particular agency
    Are event staff provided by staffing agencies insured in the UAE?

    The event staffing agency is not directly liable for providing insurance. However, it’s the responsibility of the employer to fetch the insurance of the staff members.
