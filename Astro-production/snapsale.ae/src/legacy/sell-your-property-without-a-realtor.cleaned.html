Can I sell my property in Dubai without involving a real estate agent?

Yes. You can legally sell without an agent in Dubai. Snapsale lets you skip commissions and deal directly with vetted buyers, safely and fast.

What documents do I need to sell my home in Dubai without an agent?

You’ll need:

  • Original title deed
  • Emirates ID or passport with residency
  • No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the developer
  • Mortgage clearance letter (if applicable)


These help verify ownership and clear the sale legally.

How do I know the right price to sell my home without a realtor?

Use online property tools, compare similar listings, or get a free valuation on Snapsale. We give fast, data-backed offers based on market value and demand.

Do I need to settle my mortgage before selling?

Yes, the mortgage must be cleared before ownership can be transferred. Snapsale helps you navigate the process, including getting a liability letter and bank clearance.

Are there any hidden fees when selling through Snapsale?

No hidden fees. You’ll still pay standard Dubai Land Department fees and NOC charges, but Snapsale charges no agent commissions or listing fees.

Is it safe to sell my home without a real estate agent?

Yes, if done right. Snapsale handles all checks, paperwork, and buyer verification for you.
No listings, no viewings, no scams.

How quickly can I sell my house through Snapsale?

You’ll get an offer in under 60 minutes. If accepted, we can close the deal and transfer funds within a few days.

Can I sell remotely if I’m outside the UAE?

Yes. You can appoint someone in Dubai with a Power of Attorney or work with us online.  Snapsale supports remote sellers every step of the way.
