Role of Executive Protection Officer

Protective security is the responsibility of Close Protection Dubai or a well-trained close protection officer (CPO). There is a contract that controls the relationship and geographic area of protection and defines the daily responsibilities and duties of a close personal protection officer. The main goal of CPO is to prevent any loss, damage, injury, or death to the principal. Planning and executing a strategy for keeping their client and the safety of the client’s family is the primary responsibility of a close protection officer (CPO). The CPO may conduct ongoing assessments for risks and threats.

Close security protection personnel frequently go above and beyond duty to adjust to the ever-evolving work environment and ethical standards. This is particularly true if the protection officer is also in charge of safeguarding the principal’s family. Being adaptable is essential. The most effective close protection officers integrate themselves into the principal’s and their family members’ everyday routines to provide safety and security while causing the least disturbance to their regular routines.

 

Trust with the client must be built by the protection team members. Close protection officers who demonstrate honesty in decision-making while being flexible in executing the protection mission are most successful in establishing a relationship and trust with the client.
