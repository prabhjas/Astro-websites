Bringing Your Family to the UAE: Visa Costs and Requirements

  • Sreya Sarkar
  • Updated On: November 13, 2025

Understanding the specifics of obtaining a family residence visa in the UAE, including its cost and requirements, is essential when bringing your family to the country. The UAE provides a range of visa choices for family members such as spouses, children, and parents; and the associated costs and requirements are subject to variation based on the visa type and the sponsor’s relationship to the applicant. It is important to grasp the details of acquiring a family residence visa in the UAE to ensure a smooth process.

In this article, we will explore a detailed guide on the family visa procedures; including both general and specific costs, requirements, eligibility, and additional pertinent information. Our goal is to address your most immediate concerns, guaranteeing a seamless and successful move for your family to the UAE.

Navigating Family Visas in the UAE

Family visas in the UAE are tailored to allow residents to bring their immediate family members to reside with them in the country; including spouses, children, and parents. Acquiring a family visa involves fulfilling particular criteria, including presenting proof of the relationship, financial stability, and appropriate accommodations.

Furthermore, grasping the family visa in the UAE, its price and prerequisites is essential for a successful application. The UAE provides diverse visa alternatives for family members, each with its own conditions and charges. Navigating the family visa process in the UAE demands thoughtful deliberation of these factors; in order to guarantee a seamless and compliant transition for the entire family.

Understanding Family Visa Costs UAE

Understanding the expenses related to a family residence visa in the UAE and their overall cost is crucial; as it involves considering the diverse fees linked to bringing family members into the country.

These costs may vary based on the visa type, the length of the stay, and the relationship between the applicant and the sponsor. Additionally, it is important for applicants to be aware of the visa cost in UAE and potential supplementary costs like medical insurance and Emirates ID registration.

By gaining a thorough understanding of these expenses, individuals can effectively plan and make arrangements for the process of bringing their family to the UAE.

Price Breakdown: 2 Year Family Visa in Dubai

The pricing breakdown for a 2-year family visa price in Dubai can vary depending on several factors. The general cost is influenced by the applicant’s location, the validity period of the residence visa (2 or 3 years), the desired insurance coverage for family members, and whether a normal or express service is chosen.

Additionally, this breakdown typically includes around 3500 AED for fees, including medical tests, Emirates ID, and stamping; with 200-250 AED for typing charges, and a renewal cost of approximately 3000 AED.

In 2025, the estimated fees for a two-year visa range from 3000 AED to 7000 AED. In addition, the approximate cost varies from 3550 AED without insurance to 5322 AED with insurance for applicants in Dubai; and from 2450 AED without insurance to 4022 AED with insurance for applicants outside of Dubai.

Cost of Sponsoring Parents’ Residence in Dubai

The cost of sponsoring a residence visa for parents in Dubai can vary based on several factors. The costs for sponsoring parents for a one-year stay encompass a deposit for each parent; with the total expense for issuing or renewing a residence visa for parents being approximately 359.9 AED.

Furthermore, there are additional associated expenses such as application fees at typing centers and refundable deposit fees. It is crucial to acknowledge that the overall cost may also be impacted by variables including the applicant’s location; the validity period of the residence visa (1, 2, or 3 years); and the preferred insurance coverage for family members.

Additionally, fulfilling specific requisites; such as demonstrating sole support for the parents and procuring a medical insurance policy for them; is vital for the sponsorship process. When contemplating sponsoring a residence visa for parents in Dubai, it is advisable to take into account these factors and the related costs.

Family Visa Requirements for UAE

In order to sponsor family members in the UAE, certain family visa requirements must be met.

  • Firstly, both employers and employees holding valid UAE residence visas are eligible to sponsor residence visas for their families. Employees can sponsor their families, regardless of their job titles; only if they earn a minimum salary of 4,000 AED or 3,000 AED plus accommodation. Expatriate residents are also eligible to sponsor their families for residency in the UAE, as long as they hold a valid residence permit or visa.
  • Eligibility criteria include being a UAE citizen or a valid residence visa holder, as well meeting minimum income requirements as previously mentioned. The sponsorship process can be initiated either online or offline. It is essential to prepare the necessary documents and consider accommodation requirements in advance to ensure a smooth and seamless relocation for your family to the UAE.
  • Additionally, the specific documents required for sponsoring family members depend on the individuals being sponsored. The approximate cost for issuing or renewing a residence visa for a wife and children for 3 years is around 439.9 AED. It is important to note that this cost is subject to change, and additional charges may apply.
  • The required documents to sponsor family members for a residence visa in the UAE include:
  1. Submission application, online or through an authorized typing office
  2. Employer-issued salary certificate indicating the employee’s monthly earnings
  3. Valid tenancy contract registered with the authorities
  4. Photographic evidence of the spouse and children
  5. Copies of the passports belonging to the spouse and children
  6. Medical clearance certificate for any children above the age of 18
  7. Officially attested marriage certificate
  8. Recent utility bill in the sponsor’s name
  9. Copy of the sponsor’s employment contract or company agreement

Processing Time for UAE Spouse Visa

The UAE spouse visa processing time can vary depending on several factors. The family visa procedure is expected to be completed within 4 working days in the UAE, if all required documents and qualifications are in order; to around two weeks, encompassing paperwork application and medical procedures.

It is crucial to consider that the processing time might be affected by factors such as the conclusiveness of the submitted documents; compliance with immigration regulations; and the specific protocols of the General Directorate of Residency and Foreigners Affairs (GDRFA) established in Dubai.

Therefore, individuals intending to sponsor a spouse visa in the UAE should ensure they fulfill the eligibility criteria; and that they have all essential documentation ready to facilitate a smooth and swift UAE spouse visa processing time.

Additional UAE Family Visa Considerations

When considering the family visa requirements in the UAE; it is crucial to take into account the following additional factors that offer valuable perspectives on the different aspects.

  • First, it is important to assess the eligibility for sponsorship. A non-citizen spouse, parent, or child of a UAE national holding a foreign passport can acquire a UAE residence visa; this for a duration of 5 years without being required to work in the UAE. In this scenario, the sponsor would be the UAE national.
  • Additionally, a foreign widow or divorced woman who is a mother of a UAE citizen can secure a residence visa without the need to work as well. In this instance, the sponsor would be the eldest child.
  • Then, you must take into account the visa related expenses. The cost of a family visa in Dubai may fluctuate based on factors like the type of visa, its duration, and any supplementary services or processing fees. The approximate cost for issuing or renewing a residence visa for a spouse and children for 3 years is around 439.9 AED.
  • Male expatriates must have a monthly salary of at least 4,000 AED, or 3,000 AED plus accommodation, to sponsor family members in the UAE. Female expatriates must have a minimum monthly salary of 10,000 AED or 8,000 AED along with accommodation. Wives can also sponsor their husbands but will face additional requirements from the relevant authorities.
  • All family members aged 18 and above who are applying must undergo a medical fitness examination at an approved health center. This examination includes tests for HIV and Tuberculosis.

Understanding Family Residence Visas in the UAE

In the UAE, expatriates seeking to bring their family members to live with them in the country require family residence visas. Here are some details about the process and prerequisites for acquiring family residence visas in the UAE:

  • The expatriate or their employer can sponsor family visas for the UAE. Which means you must file the UAE Family Visa application within 60 days of obtaining your own UAE Residence Visa; and you can submit it at the GDRFA offices of the relevant Emirate.
  • Additionally, you must consider the shared cancellation and Grace Period related to the family visa. This indicates that the family’s residence permits are linked to the sponsoring family member’s residence permit; thus, the dependents’ visas need to be cancelled if the sponsoring family member’s visa is cancelled. Dependents have a 6-month grace period from the visa’s expiry or cancellation date to secure a new residence permit; neglecting to renew or cancel dependents’ visas may lead to fines.

Costs Associated with Family Residence Visas

The family residence visa in the UAE and the cost related to it may fluctuate depending on various factors. Here’s an overview of the expenses involved:

  • In the UAE, male sponsors must have a minimum salary of 4,000 AED, or 3,000 AED along with accommodations. On the other hand, female UAE residents wishing to sponsor their families in the UAE need to have a minimum salary of 8,000 AED along with accommodations, or 10,000 AED.
  • In addition, you need to pay 100 AED to renew an Emirates ID for one year, and the fee for visa stamping, including delivery charges, amounts to 500 AED.
  • Finally, the family visa application fee is 310 AED, and you need to pay a refundable deposit of 1,000 AED.

It is crucial to emphasize that the family residency visa in the UAE and the cost linked to it may comprise different elements; including visa application fees, refundable deposits, medical assessments, and health insurance.

Other potential costs may include:

  • The entry permits vary in cost, ranging from 400 AED to 1200 AED; while the price of the visa status change is 690 AED.
  • Moreover, medical tests can amount to approximately 300 to 450 AED, and health insurance typically starts at 450 AED per month. It is also important to consider that using a typing center or agent for the visa application may result in extra service charges.
  • Furthermore, the typical processing period for a Dubai family visa is 15 to 17 days; although in exceptional circumstances it may extend to 60 days. Not to mention, family visas in the UAE are available for 1, 2, or 3 years and can be renewed before they expire.

Requirements for Sponsoring Family Members

Expatriate residents, including both employers and employees, have the opportunity to sponsor their families to live in the UAE; provided they possess a valid residence permit or visa. They must also fulfill specific requirements that delineate the essential conditions and procedures for sponsoring family members in the UAE.

  • Male expatriates in the UAE must have a minimum salary of 4,000 AED, or 3,000 AED along with accommodations to sponsor their family members. Differently, female UAE residents need to have a minimum salary of 10,000 AED, or 8,000 AED along with accommodations.
  • Lastly, for babies born in the UAE, they must have a residence visa applied for within 120 days of their birth to avoid penalties. Expatriate residents can also sponsor their stepchildren, with conditions established by the GDRFA. The residence visas for stepchildren have a duration of one year and are eligible for annual renewal.
  • To sponsor a wife and children, the necessary documents include an online application or submission through a registered typing office. Furthermore, sponsors must ensure that both male and female family members over the age of 18 pass medical fitness tests at approved health centers in the UAE.
