Commonly Asked FAQs for Freelancers

Is Freelancing Legal in UAE?

Yes it is. With a freelance visa, work permits, and a resident visa you can legally work as a freelancer in the region. The United Arab Emirates is one of the greatest places in the world for freelance workers. Subsequently, becoming a freelancer is a great idea for expats and locals alike. Due to policy changes by the UAE government, freelancing is safer than ever. It is a great alternative to receive a side income. Additionally, freelancers do not need to occupy office space. They usually work from their comfort spaces. Many great corporations are rejoicing at the opportunity to fill their workforce in a cost-efficient way

Who qualifies for a freelance visa?

Anyone who wants to start freelancing in the area can apply for both a visa and a freelance permit. A bachelor’s degree or specialist diploma is the main requirement of the principal applicant. They must also demonstrate that they have earned at least 360,000 AED in the two most recent years.

What are the basic requirements for a Freelance Visa?

• Passport copy

• Emirates ID (for those who have previously had a UAE visa)

• Visa page copy (for those who have previously had a UAE visa)

• Attested degree (if available)

• Entry stamp or visit visa paper (if available)

• Visa cancellation paper (for those who have previously had a UAE visa)

• Digital passport photo

Can I sponsor my family through freelance work visa?

Yes. As long as they have a current residency status, employers and employees are able to sponsor their relatives in the UAE.

How long will it take to get a Freelance Visa?

Freelance Visa process can take up to 25 working days. However, we offer a VIP process as well for those who need to leave the country for business or personal purposes. VIP process costs 1,150 AED on top of the package.

Can I work in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, or any other emirate while having a Dubai Freelance Visa?

Yes, having a freelance visa from a mainland company like Connect Freelance allows you to work in different Emirates. For example, some people choose to work in Abu Dhabi due to lower rent costs there. Irrespective of the free zone an individual applies for the visa, they can work all across the UAE in their specific field.

Do I need to pay vat as a freelancer?

Yes. Freelancers should know that they must register for VAT and obey VAT laws if their operation provides an economic gain to them.

Is it possible to engage in freelance work in the UAE while simultaneously being employed by a company?

While working in the UAE, individuals are allowed to engage in freelance work, but the employer, who is likely also the person who sponsored the residence visa, must grant an authorization via a NOC.

Am I eligible to apply for a driving license under a Freelance Visa?

Yes, you can apply for a driving license under a freelance visa. To make this process as easier as possible you can partner with an agency to obtain your driver’s license. They will help you obtain the necessary documentation such as the Non-Objection Certificate and more. Additionally, they will guide you through every step, from requiring the permit to providing you with the resources necessary for the test.

Can I work with clients outside UAE with this Freelance Visa?

Yes, individuals can work with a freelance visa with clients inside and outside of the UAE. They usually accept assignments that provide them the opportunity to work remotely. Consequently, working with foreign clients is easier than it may seem. Also, they can leave the region for six months if they need to.

Can I apply for Freelance Visa while I’m outside the UAE?

Yes, you can. When applying for a freelance visa from outside the UAE the process will follow the same path as if you were applying from inside the country. The only difference is that you will have to enter the UAE with the E-visa we will provide you with. Once you have entered the country we can process with the change status and continue with the steps.

What is the renewal process for Freelance Visa?

The renewal process for the freelance visa is a procedure that will allow you to extend your 2-year visa for more time. Price may vary depending on the government fees but as you we speak it will be as the same package you bought the visa for.

What is the “freelance permit“ in the UAE?

A work permit in Dubai is also known as a freelance permission or a freelance license. It makes it possible for people to work for themselves as independent contractors or without being long-term employees of any particular corporation.

What should I do if my freelance visa application is rejected?

If your initial application for a visa to Dubai and the UAE was denied, you can try again. Before reapplying, it is advisable that you first determine the cause of the denial and take action to resolve the problem.

Can I open a personal bank account if I have a Freelance Visa?

Yes. Opening a personal savings bank account is possible under a freelance visa. It is required for freelancers to have a bank account to receive the earnings from their hard work. To open a bank account, you will need your Emirates ID Card, your original passport plus a copy of your visa, and a salary certificate which the company will assist you with.

How long can I be outside the UAE if I have this Freelance Visa?

You can only be outside of the United Arab Emirates for six months if you are under a freelance visa. If you do not enter the region for a longer period, your UAE visa and residency will become invalid. If it does happen, you will be obligated to go through the freelancer visa process one more time to work as a freelancer in the region again.

What are the requirements of sponsoring a family?

To sponsor their families, freelance visa owners must:

  • Have residency permit.
  • Minimum wage of: 4,000 AED, or 3,000 AED plus accommodation.
  • Submit the sponsorship application in less than 60 days after receiving the residency visa.
Am I allowed to travel in and out of the UAE while holding a Freelance Visa?

Yes, those with a freelancing visa are able to enter and exit the UAE without any issues. However, they must take care to avoid leaving the area for over 6 months at once.

Freelance Permit vs. Freelance Visa: What’s the Difference?

You are able to work lawfully in the UAE as a freelancer thanks to the permit. The visa, however, enables you to reside in the United Arab Emirates and, in some circumstances, to sponsor family members.

What are the job opportunities under Freelance Visa?

Freelance visa holders have many opportunities in the UAE, these include:

  • Marketing.
  • Web development.
  • IT support.
  • Consultancy.
  • Photography.
  • Modeling.

  • Fitness training and more.

The freelance visa is an authorization that lets you to operate lawfully as a freelancer or contractor in Dubai UAE, with the option of sponsoring your family. To obtain one you must follow certain processes that include filling the application; providing your documents; passing a medical exam, and more. In the UAE, where can I get freelance work? Can I operate outside the free zone?

Yes, individuals from anywhere in the world can work as freelancers in UAE as long as they have their freelance permits and visas. The Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratization (MoHRE) has announced a new freelance permit that will be made available in 2023 for individuals of any skill level who wish to apply for freelance work in the United Arab Emirates. They are able to work from any part of the world or the United Arab Emirates.

What documents do I need to renew my freelance visa in UAE? Do I need a bank statement or an audit report?

To renew a freelance visa in the UAE, certain documents must be presented to the authorities. These documents include a valid passport, a current residence visa, a passport size photograph. Additionally, the freelancer must prove that they have the financial means to support themselves while in the UAE. As a freelancer, you do not have to provide your financial records for an annual audit by the government. Again, keeping proper records is advised in the event this status changes in the future.

How do I pay for my freelance visa once it’s approved in the UAE?

Once your freelance visa application has been approved in the UAE, you will need to pay the applicable fees in order to receive the visa. Depending on the type of freelance visa you have applied for, the fees may vary. Generally, the fees for a freelance visa include an application fee, a visa issuance fee, and a security deposit. The application fee is the amount you need to pay in order to submit your application. This fee is usually paid up front and is non-refundable. You can pay these fees in cash or by using a credit card. It is important to note that the fees are subject to change, so it is important to check with the relevant authorities before making your payment.

Do I get a refund if my freelance visa application in UAE is rejected?

No, the UAE’s immigration officials do not reimburse applicants for their visa expenses when their applications get rejected.
