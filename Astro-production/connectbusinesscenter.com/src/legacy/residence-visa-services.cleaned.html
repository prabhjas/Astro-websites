Residence Visa in UAE

The UAE is one of the top destinations for investors, entrepreneurs, students, professionals, and more from around the globe. As a result, the local government is constantly providing new residency schemes for foreigners looking to move to the area. With a UAE residence visa, outsiders can legally live, work, and study in the region.

Eligibility Criteria for Residence Visa in Dubai

There are certain criteria that foreigners have to follow to qualify for a residence visa in Dubai. To be eligible to obtain a residency visa in the UAE, you must:

  • Have employment in the UAE under a public or private company.
  • Be a student in a local educational institution.
    Invest in the UAE.
  • Be a relative of a UAE national or international resident.
  • Buy a property in the UAE.
  • Want to retire in the UAE.

Required Documents for Residence Visa Application

If you are looking to fill a residence visa application, you must provide the following documents:

  • Valid passport.
  • Headshot pictures.
  • Entry visa.
  • Sponsor’s UAE citizenship or legal residency proof.
  • Medical fitness certificate.
  • UAE health insurance.
  • Family ties (birth or marriage certificates) if necessary.
  • Contract copies and valid trade license copies of the company employing you (if necessary).

Renewal and Extension of Residence Visa

Your sponsor must make sure to renew your residence visa before it expires. The usual time for renewal of this visa is 30 days. However, if this time passes and the sponsor does not renew it, the visa holder has a grace period to do so. This grace period lasts for 6 months and it allows holders to have an extra period to extend their visa.

The process for renewal is easy to the procedure to obtain it for the first time. With our professional support, you will be able to renew the visa and not worry about missing your expiry date.

What is a residence visa in Dubai and why do I need it?

The UAE residency visa is a permission that allows foreigners to stay in the region for longer periods of time. It is necessary if you want to reside in the region. Therefore, if you are looking to work, study, start a business, or live in the UAE, you must obtain a residence visa.

What are the eligibility criteria for obtaining a residence visa in Dubai?
  • Be employed by a governmental or private company in the UAE.
  • Enroll as a student at a nearby college or university.
    Invest in the UAE.
  • Be a close relative of an international or national resident of the UAE.
  • Purchase a property in the UAE.
  • Considering retiring in the UAE.
What documents are required for a residence visa application in Dubai?

The necessary documents for the visa application include: passport, passport-sized pictures, medical certificate, sponsor’s citizenship proof, entry visa, and more. Requirements can vary depending on the type of residency visa you will be applying for.

How long does it take to process a residence visa application in Dubai?

This can vary depending on the visa application and type of residence visa; but, it usually takes between 5 to 10 working days.

Can I work in Dubai with a residence visa?

Yes, people can work in the UAE with a residency visa. The ability to legally work in the region is one of the main benefits of having this type of visa.

How can I renew or extend my residence visa in Dubai?

Your sponsor is in charge of renewing your visa before it expires. If they do not follow suit, they will have a grace period to do so. They can renew or extend it through the official authorities’ website by themselves or they can hire a visa services agency to do so.
