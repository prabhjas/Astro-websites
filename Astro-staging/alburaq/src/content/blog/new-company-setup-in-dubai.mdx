Setting up a new company in Dubai is one of the most exciting steps an entrepreneur can take. The city offers world-class infrastructure, tax benefits, and access to global markets — but the setup process requires navigating several regulatory steps.

## Choose Your Business Structure

### Mainland Company (LLC)
An LLC allows you to operate anywhere in the UAE and bid on government contracts. Post-2021 reforms now allow 100% foreign ownership in most sectors on the mainland.

### Free Zone Company
Over 40 free zones in the UAE offer 100% foreign ownership, zero import/export duties, and simplified setup. Best for businesses focused on international trade.

### Offshore Company
For holding structures, intellectual property ownership, and international business without a UAE presence.

## Step-by-Step Setup Process

1. **Define your business activity** — choose from UAE's approved activity list
2. **Select a legal structure** — LLC, FZE, FZC, sole proprietorship, branch
3. **Choose a location** — mainland DED, specific free zone, or offshore jurisdiction
4. **Reserve your trade name** — check availability and comply with naming rules
5. **Apply for initial approval** — government confirmation you can proceed
6. **Prepare MOA/AOA** — Memorandum and Articles of Association
7. **Lease office space** — required for mainland; virtual offices available in some free zones
8. **Obtain your trade license** — the final step that makes your company legal
9. **Open a corporate bank account** — UAE has many local and international banking options
10. **Register for UAE VAT** — mandatory if annual turnover exceeds AED 375,000
