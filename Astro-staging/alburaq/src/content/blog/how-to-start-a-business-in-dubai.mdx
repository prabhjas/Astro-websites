# How to Start a Business in Dubai

Starting a business in Dubai is one of the most rewarding decisions an entrepreneur can make...

## Step 1: Choose Your Business Activity

Dubai offers hundreds of approved business activities across mainland and free zones.

## Step 2: Select Your Legal Structure

- **LLC** – most common for mainland businesses
- **Free Zone Company** – 100% foreign ownership, tax benefits
- **Branch Office** – for extending a foreign company into Dubai
