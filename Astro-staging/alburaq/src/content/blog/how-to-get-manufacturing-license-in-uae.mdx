Manufacturing in the UAE offers significant advantages — strategic location, world-class logistics infrastructure, and access to GCC markets without customs duties. Obtaining a manufacturing license is the first step to establishing your production operation in the UAE.

## Who Needs a Manufacturing License?

Any business engaged in:
- Production or assembly of physical goods
- Food and beverage processing
- Packaging and labeling operations
- Industrial or chemical manufacturing
- Construction materials production

## Manufacturing License Requirements in the UAE

### Core Requirements
1. **Approved business activity** — from the UAE's industrial activity list
2. **Industrial land or approved facility** — manufacturing requires a physical production space in an industrial zone
3. **Initial approval** — from DED (mainland) or the relevant free zone authority
4. **Environmental permit** — from the UAE Ministry of Climate Change and Environment (if applicable)
5. **Dubai Municipality approval** — for food-related manufacturing
6. **Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology (MOIAT)** — registration for industrial businesses

### Facility Requirements
- Minimum facility size varies by activity (typically 1,000–5,000 sq ft for light manufacturing)
- Must be in an approved industrial zone (not residential or commercial)
- Must meet UAE fire safety and building codes

## Costs of Getting a Manufacturing License

| Cost Component | Approximate Range (AED) |
|---|---|
| Trade license fee | 10,000 – 25,000 |
| Industrial facility lease | 40,000 – 200,000/year |
| Municipality fees | 2,000 – 8,000 |
| Environmental approvals | 3,000 – 15,000 |
| Initial approvals | 5,000 – 20,000 |

Total first-year costs typically range from AED 60,000 to AED 250,000+ depending on activity and facility size.

## Best Locations for Manufacturing in the UAE

**Dubai Industrial City** — dedicated industrial city with excellent logistics connectivity
**Dubai South** — near Al Maktoum Airport, ideal for export-oriented manufacturing
**Sharjah Industrial Areas** — cost-effective alternative with proximity to Dubai
**Free Zone Options** — JAFZA, Dubai Industrial Park, KIZAD (Abu Dhabi) offer 100% foreign ownership with specific industrial mandates
