Choosing the wrong business license in Dubai is one of the most common and costly mistakes new entrepreneurs make. The UAE has a well-defined licensing system, but navigating it requires understanding your specific business needs, activity type, and operational goals.

## The Four Main License Types in Dubai

### 1. Commercial License
For businesses engaged in trading activities — buying and selling goods, import/export, distribution. If your revenue comes from the sale of physical products, this is your license.

**Who needs it:** Retailers, wholesalers, traders, importers, exporters

### 2. Professional License
For service-based businesses where the primary value is expertise or skill. Professionals, consultants, and service providers fall under this category.

**Who needs it:** Management consultants, IT service providers, marketers, HR specialists, trainers

### 3. Industrial License
For manufacturing, production, and industrial operations. Requires a warehouse or factory facility.

**Who needs it:** Manufacturers, food producers, assemblers, industrial service providers

### 4. Tourism License
Specifically for businesses in the travel and hospitality sector — travel agencies, tour operators, hotel apartments.

## Mainland vs Free Zone Licensing

**Mainland License (DED)**
- Can operate anywhere in the UAE
- Can bid on government contracts
- Requires a physical office in the relevant emirate
- Post-2021: 100% foreign ownership in most activities

**Free Zone License**
- 100% foreign ownership (has always been available)
- Cannot operate in the UAE mainland without a distribution agent
- Ideal for import/export and international businesses
- Simpler setup in many cases

## Key Questions to Ask

1. Where will your customers be? (UAE mainland vs international)
2. Do you need a physical office address?
3. Will you hire UAE employees or bring in expatriate staff?
4. Do you plan to bid on government contracts?
5. What is your primary revenue source — goods or services?
