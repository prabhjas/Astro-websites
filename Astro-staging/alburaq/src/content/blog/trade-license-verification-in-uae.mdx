Before entering any business partnership or signing contracts in the UAE, verifying the trade license of the other party is essential. License verification protects you from fraud, ensures your partner is legally authorized to operate, and confirms the business details are accurate.

## Why Trade License Verification Matters

The UAE has strict business laws, but fraudulent companies do attempt to operate without valid licenses. Verifying a license confirms:
- The business is legally registered
- The license is current and not expired
- The business is authorized for the activities they claim
- The registered address and owner details are accurate

## How to Verify a UAE Trade License

### Dubai (DED)
Visit the Dubai Economy portal at **ded.ae** and use the business search function. Enter the company name or license number to view current status, activities, and expiry date.

### Abu Dhabi (ADBC)
Use the Abu Dhabi Business Center (ADBC) portal to verify licenses issued in Abu Dhabi emirate.

### Free Zone Verification
Each free zone has its own verification portal. Check directly with the relevant free zone authority — JAFZA, DIFC, DMCC, etc. — using their online license verification tools.

### Federal Level
The Ministry of Economy's business registry allows federal-level verification of company status.

## What to Check During Verification

1. **License number and expiry date** — is the license current?
2. **Business activities** — are they licensed for what they claim to offer?
3. **Legal name and trade name** — matches documents and invoices?
4. **Registered address** — matches the location you're dealing with?
5. **Ownership structure** — who are the registered owners?

## When to Verify

Always verify before: signing contracts, making payments, entering joint ventures, and appointing new suppliers or clients.
