Dubai has emerged as one of the world's most attractive destinations for foreign entrepreneurs. The combination of tax benefits, strategic location, world-class infrastructure, and a business-friendly regulatory environment makes it uniquely compelling.

## Why Dubai Attracts Foreign Entrepreneurs

### Zero Personal Income Tax
Dubai levies no personal income tax. Your salary, dividends, and investment returns are yours to keep entirely. This is a major advantage over most Western jurisdictions.

### Low Corporate Tax
The UAE introduced a 9% corporate tax in 2023 (effective for fiscal years starting June 2023), but with a AED 375,000 exemption threshold. Free zone businesses meeting substance requirements continue to enjoy 0% corporate tax.

### 100% Foreign Ownership
Since 2021, the UAE allows 100% foreign ownership in most mainland business activities, eliminating the need for UAE national partners in most sectors.

### Strategic Global Location
Dubai sits at the crossroads of Europe, Asia, and Africa. Within 8 hours' flight, you can reach 2/3 of the world's population. Dubai International Airport is one of the world's busiest.

### World-Class Infrastructure
From internet connectivity to transport to ports to banking — Dubai's infrastructure rivals the best in the world. This makes operations efficient and internationally competitive.

### Access to GCC Markets
A Dubai presence gives you immediate access to the $2 trillion+ GCC economy across 6 countries. Many multinationals use Dubai as their regional HQ for the entire Middle East and Africa.

### Ease of Doing Business
Dubai consistently ranks in the top 20 globally for ease of doing business. Company setup can be completed in days, not months.

### Quality of Life
Excellent healthcare, education, safety, and lifestyle make Dubai attractive for both business owners and the high-quality international talent they want to hire.

## Common Foreign Business Setup Options

- **Free Zone Company** — 100% ownership, international trade focus
- **Mainland LLC** — UAE market access, government contracts
- **Branch Office** — extension of your existing foreign company
