For decades, foreign companies doing business in the UAE mainland required a UAE national (local sponsor) as a partner or service agent. While recent reforms have expanded areas where 100% foreign ownership is permitted, many business activities and specific sectors still require — or benefit from — a local sponsor arrangement.

## What Is a Local Sponsor?

A local sponsor (also called a local service agent or local partner) is a UAE national who either:
- **Holds a minority share** in your company (typically 51%), in sectors requiring UAE national ownership
- **Acts as a service agent** (for professional licenses) without equity participation, receiving an annual fee for facilitating government interactions

## Types of Sponsorship Arrangements

### Corporate Sponsor (Nominee Partner)
The UAE national holds shares in your company on paper but does not participate in management or profit. Side agreements protect the foreign investor's operational control and financial interests.

### Individual Sponsor
A UAE individual (rather than a corporate entity) acts as the local partner or service agent.

### Silent Sponsor
A local partner who receives a fixed annual fee but has no involvement in day-to-day operations.

## What Does a Good Local Sponsor Do?

An effective local sponsor:
- Facilitates interactions with UAE government authorities
- Uses their network and standing to expedite approvals
- Represents the business credibly in local dealings
- Maintains good standing with relevant government departments
