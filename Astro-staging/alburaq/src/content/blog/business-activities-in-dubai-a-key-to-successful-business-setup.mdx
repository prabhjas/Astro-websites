When setting up a business in Dubai, choosing the correct business activity is one of the most critical decisions you'll make. Your business activity determines which license you need, which authority regulates you, and where you can operate.

## What Are Business Activities in Dubai?

The UAE Department of Economic Development (DED) maintains an approved list of over 2,000 business activities. Each activity falls under one of four main license types:
- Commercial License
- Professional License
- Industrial License
- Tourism License

## Why Business Activity Selection Matters

Choosing the wrong activity leads to:
- Operating outside your licensed scope (a legal violation)
- Needing additional approvals from sector-specific authorities
- Difficulty in opening a business bank account
- Potential fines and operational shutdowns

## How to Choose the Right Business Activity

### Step 1: Define What You Actually Do
Be specific. "Consulting" is too broad. "Management Consulting" or "HR Consulting" or "IT Consulting" are distinct activities with different requirements.

### Step 2: Match to DED's Activity List
Cross-reference your actual services with the official DED activity list. Some activities require preliminary approval from sector authorities (e.g., healthcare needs Dubai Health Authority approval).

### Step 3: Consider Multiple Activities
You can register multiple activities under one license. This provides flexibility as your business grows.

### Step 4: Check Free Zone Restrictions
Each free zone has an approved list of activities. Not all activities are available in all free zones.

## Common Activity Categories

**Trading:** Import, export, retail distribution
**Services:** Consulting, IT, marketing, logistics
**Manufacturing:** Production, assembly, packaging
**Professional:** Engineering, legal, medical, architectural
